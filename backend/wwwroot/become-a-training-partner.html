Training Partners

Become a PCI Training Partner

PCI Training Partners deliver PCL-AI exam preparation — courses, workshops and study support that help candidates get ready for the examination. The examination itself and the certification decision remain fully independent of any training provider. No partner sets or marks the exam, and no partner can influence who passes. That independence is deliberate: it is what keeps the PCL-AI credential impartial and trustworthy, and every partner is expected to represent it honestly.

Project Controls Institute Global, Inc. is a Delaware Non-Stock Corporation. It intends to seek recognition as a tax-exempt nonprofit organisation under Section 501(c)(3) of the U.S. Internal Revenue Code; tax-exempt status has not yet been granted, and contributions are not currently represented as tax-deductible. PCI is not currently accredited by ANAB, IAS, or any ISO/IEC 17024 accreditation body — its certification framework is being developed with reference to ISO/IEC 17024 personnel-certification principles. PCI credentials can be verified at any time through the public credential lookup. PCL-AI is not government-recognised, ISO-accredited or formally accredited.
