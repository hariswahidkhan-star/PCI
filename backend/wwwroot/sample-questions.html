Sample questions.

Illustrative examples only. Live examination items are confidential, validated and secured. These samples are written purely to show format and are not scored or representative of difficulty mix.

A schedule shows the critical path running through a procurement activity with no float. The most appropriate first action is to:

A. Add a positive lag to absorb the risk
B. Examine the activity’s logic and duration assumptions
C. Increase the project contingency
D. Re-baseline the schedule
Answer: B
The critical path should first be interrogated for logic and duration validity before changing contingency or re-baselining.

CPI = 0.85 and SPI = 1.05 on a project. This indicates work is:

A. Ahead of schedule and under budget
B. Behind schedule and over budget
C. Ahead of schedule but over budget
D. Behind schedule but under budget
Answer: C
SPI>1 indicates ahead of schedule; CPI<1 indicates over budget (costing more than the value earned).

The primary purpose of a cost-estimate classification (e.g., Class 5 to Class 1) is to communicate:

A. The contingency percentage
B. The maturity and expected accuracy of the estimate
C. The contract type
D. The funding source
Answer: B
Estimate classes signal the level of project definition and the expected accuracy range of the estimate.

In quantitative schedule-risk analysis, a Monte Carlo simulation primarily produces:

A. A single deterministic finish date
B. A probability distribution of possible outcomes
C. The critical path
D. The resource histogram
Answer: B
Monte Carlo sampling yields a distribution (e.g., P50/P80 dates), not a single deterministic value.

An AI tool forecasts a more optimistic completion date than the analyst’s manual forecast. Under PCI’s governed-AI principle, the professional should:

A. Adopt the AI forecast, as it uses more data
B. Discard the AI output
C. Investigate the drivers of the difference and apply professional judgement before deciding
D. Average the two figures
Answer: C
“AI proposes, the professional disposes”: AI augments judgement. The difference should be understood and a reasoned decision made — not blindly adopted or ignored.

Which practice best protects the integrity of reported project-controls data?

A. Allowing each team to keep its own spreadsheet
B. Maintaining a single source of truth with controlled inputs
C. Reporting only favourable metrics
D. Re-keying data manually each period
Answer: B
A controlled single source of truth reduces error and manipulation and underpins trustworthy reporting.

Study method

How to use these samples well

Six questions can't measure readiness, but used deliberately they can sharpen it. The value isn't in getting them right — it's in watching how you get them right.

  • Answer before you peek. Commit to an option and write one sentence of reasoning before checking. The reasoning, not the letter, is what the exam tests.
  • Diagnose misses by domain. A wrong earned-value answer and a wrong scheduling answer call for different revision. Map every miss to a domain of the Body of Knowledge and study there.
  • Re-derive, don't memorise. These exact items will not appear. Being able to explain why B is right — and why A, C and D are wrong — is the transferable skill.
Item styles

What each style of question is testing

Concept & definition

Do you know what a term means precisely enough to distinguish near-neighbours — estimate class from contingency, float from lag?

Calculation & interpretation

Can you compute an index or variance and, more importantly, say what it means for the project? Numbers without interpretation earn nothing in practice.

Scenario judgement

Given a realistic situation, can you pick the most appropriate first action? These reward disciplined method — interrogate before you escalate.

Governed-AI judgement

When an AI output and professional analysis disagree, do you know the professional's obligation? These test “AI proposes, the professional disposes” in applied form.
