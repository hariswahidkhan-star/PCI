Terms of Enrolment.

The terms that govern PCI student membership and enrolment. Please read these alongside the Privacy Policy and applicable PCI candidate and member policies.

What enrolment provides

Enrolment provides student membership access and access to the PCI student panel. Certification, examination, credential award, and registry listing are subject to separate eligibility, assessment, verification, and applicable PCI policies.

Founding-stage status

PCI is a professional certification institute. Its framework is being developed with reference to ISO/IEC 17024 personnel-certification principles. PCI is not currently ISO/IEC 17024 accredited.

Payment, refunds and cancellation

Fees and the terms attached to them are confirmed at the point of purchase. Payment is processed by a secure third-party payment gateway; PCI does not store raw card details. Refund and cancellation eligibility depends on the membership purchased and the circumstances, and is handled in line with the terms presented at checkout. Where a payment is not completed, membership is not activated.

Your account and security

On successful payment, a student account is created and you are sent a secure link to set your own password. For security, PCI does not email plain permanent passwords. You are responsible for keeping your login details confidential and for the activity on your account.

Acceptable use

Student membership and panel access are for your individual professional use. Resources provided through the panel are made available for your own learning and preparation and may not be redistributed.

Certification is separate

Membership does not grant certification, exam pass status, accreditation, or credential registry listing. Certification, examination, credential award and registry listing are subject to separate eligibility, assessment, verification and applicable PCI policies, including the candidate handbook, code of ethics, and examination policies.

Changes

PCI is a institute and its programmes are developing. These terms, membership benefits and pricing may be updated; material changes will be communicated through the institute’s usual channels.

Common questions

Does paying make me certified?

No — enrolment provides student membership and panel access only. Certification is separate and subject to assessment and PCI policies.

Are these terms the full agreement?

These terms apply together with the Privacy Policy and the applicable PCI candidate and member policies referenced throughout the site.

How do I cancel?

Contact support; cancellation is handled in line with the terms presented at checkout for your membership.

Your side

Responsibilities of an enrolled student

Enrolment is an agreement with obligations on both sides. PCI's are set out across these terms; yours are few, but they matter:

  • Provide accurate information at registration and keep your details current
  • Keep your login confidential — access is personal and not for sharing
  • Use panel materials for your own learning; do not redistribute them
  • Describe your status accurately: “PCI student member” is correct; anything implying certification is not
  • Meet the conduct expectations that apply to all members under membership conduct

None of this is onerous — but breaches, particularly misrepresentation of status or redistribution of materials, are taken seriously because they affect every other member's trust in the framework.

Practical questions

Renewal, lapse and what you may say

What happens if I do not renew?

Access to student membership and the panel ends at the close of your paid term; nothing further is charged. Records are retained only as long as PCI's retention rules require, and you can re-enrol later — a lapse is not held against you.

What can I put on my CV or LinkedIn as a student member?

Exactly what you are: a student member of the Project Controls Institute, preparing for the PCP-AI if that is the case. Do not use wording that implies certification, credential award or registry listing before they have happened.

Can PCI change these terms after I enrol?

The terms can be updated as the programme develops, and material changes are communicated through the institute's usual channels before they take effect. The version published on this site is the version in force.
