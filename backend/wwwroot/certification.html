It is not a generic AI badge and not a project-management certificate — it is a modern project-controls credential.

Why it exists

The discipline outgrew its credentials.

Project controls decides whether the world's largest investments succeed — yet the field has lacked a single, rigorous credential covering the whole discipline and the AI now reshaping it. Generic project-management certificates don't go deep on controls; vendor courses teach tools, not judgement. The PCP-AI exists to close that gap.

A whole-discipline standard

One credential across planning, cost, earned value, forecasting, risk and finance — not a fragment.

Built for the age of AI

A dedicated AI knowledge area in every section, always governed by a human.

Independent & rigorous

Criterion-referenced, ISO/IEC 17024-referenced, and owned by no vendor.

Competency framework

Twelve competencies, one professional.

The PCP-AI certifies a connected competency model — the full controls discipline, with AI governance as a first-class competency in its own right.

01

Project Management

Integrating scope, time, cost and risk so a project is planned, controlled and delivered as one coherent whole — not a set of disconnected workstreams.

02

Planning & Scheduling

Building realistic, logic-linked schedules and critical paths, and keeping them credible as the work and the conditions change.

03

Cost Estimating

Producing defensible cost estimates, from order-of-magnitude to definitive, with transparent assumptions, bases and allowances.

04

Cost Control

Tracking commitments, actuals and value of work done against budget, and explaining variances before they become surprises.

05

Forecasting

Projecting final cost and completion honestly from current performance, so leaders see where a project is heading, not just where it has been.

06

Earned Value

Measuring progress objectively by integrating scope, schedule and cost, and reading the indices to know whether a project is genuinely on track.

07

Risk Management

Identifying, quantifying and managing threats and opportunities, and reflecting them in schedules, costs and contingency.

08

Commercial Management

Managing contracts, change, claims and the commercial relationships that decide whether project value is protected or eroded.

09

Project Finance

Understanding how projects are funded and financed, and how cash flow, drawdowns and financing terms shape delivery decisions.

10

Financial Awareness

Connecting project performance to the organisation’s financial position — margins, capital and the numbers leadership and investors actually watch.

11

Reporting & Analytics

Turning data into clear, trustworthy reporting and insight that helps decision-makers act, rather than dashboards no one can interpret.

12

AI Governance

Applying AI to controls work responsibly — validating, explaining and owning AI-assisted outputs so judgement and accountability stay with a competent human.

Examination blueprint

How the exam is weighted.

The PCP-AI is assessed across all ten sections together. Each section currently carries an even 10% — a deliberate starting design; a formal job-task analysis will confirm or redistribute the weighting before the exam blueprint is fixed (ISO/IEC 17024). AI is examined in every section — each carries its own dedicated AI knowledge area — as well as in the dedicated AI & Data Science core.

01 · Project management10%
02 · Planning & scheduling10%
03 · Cost estimating & budgeting10%
04 · Cost control & forecasting10%
05 · Earned value & performance10%
06 · Risk management & ERM10%
07 · Commercial, contracts & finance10%
08 · Financial & management accounting10%
09 · Reporting, data & systems10%
10 · AI & data science core10%

An even 10% per section is a deliberate starting structure; item counts and final weighting will be confirmed by a formal job-task analysis before the exam is built, in line with ISO/IEC 17024.

Common questions

What is the PCP-AI?

The Certified Project Controls Professional – AI (PCP-AI) is PCI's credential. It certifies demonstrated competence across the integrated project-controls discipline — from planning and cost control to AI governance — through an independent, scenario-based assessment.

Who is it for?

Practising controls professionals — planners, schedulers, cost engineers, risk and controls managers, PMO staff — and capable people moving into the field. The entry requirement is based on relevant professional experience (around three years) rather than a specific qualification.

Is it accredited?

PCI is building toward formal accreditation rather than claiming it. The credential and its processes are designed around the ISO/IEC 17024 framework. We state our status plainly and do not imply recognition we do not yet hold — that honesty is part of how a credible certification body earns trust.

How is it different from PMP or AACE certifications?

It certifies the whole controls discipline as one integrated competence, and treats AI governance as a core part of it rather than an add-on — built for how controls work is done now. PCI does not claim equivalence with, or endorsement from, other certification bodies.

Does any course guarantee a pass?

No. The certification decision is independent and based on your assessed competence, not attendance on any course — and no preparation guarantees a pass. That separation between training and assessment is what makes the credential credible.
