Common questions

Does enrolment mean I am certified?

No. Enrolment gives you student membership and access to the PCI student panel. Certification, examination, credential award and registry listing are subject to separate eligibility, assessment, verification and applicable PCI policies.

How much is membership?

The student membership / registration fee is USD 99. A current 50% membership discount applies, bringing the amount payable today to USD 49.50. This is a one-time payment with no instalment options.

How much is the PCP-AI exam?

The PCP-AI certification exam fee is USD 500, with a current 30% discount bringing the amount payable to USD 350 for eligible candidates. Exam payment provides access to the applicable exam registration process; it is separate from membership.

Is there a bundle?

Yes. The Membership + Exam Bundle combines membership (USD 49.50) and the exam (USD 350) for a total payable today of USD 399.50.

What is the recertification / renewal fee?

The recertification / renewal fee is USD 99 per three-year renewal cycle, subject to applicable PCI renewal and membership requirements. Keeping membership active may be required to maintain access to member resources and applicable credential services.

Is there a retake fee?

The exam retake fee is to be confirmed and will be displayed before any exam retake booking is completed.

Are there instalment or payment-plan options?

No. All fees are paid as a single one-time payment; there are no instalment, payment-plan, or financing options.

What happens after payment?

Your student account is activated and you are sent a secure free student account first — no payment needed. Build your profile in the student portal, then activate membership and pay exam fees from your dashboard whenever you are ready.

Can I save my progress and finish later?

Yes. The enrolment wizard begins with your email so your progress is saved after every step. You can return later using the same email address and continue from where you left off.

Is the payment secure?

Yes. Payment is handled by a secure, PCI-DSS-compliant payment gateway, verified before access is activated. PCI does not store raw card details on its own servers.

Is the certification currently accredited?

PCI is a professional certification institute. Its framework is being developed with reference to ISO/IEC 17024 personnel-certification principles. PCI is not currently ISO/IEC 17024 accredited.
