Community

The PCI discussion forum

Open to everyone — practitioners, candidates and the curious. Ask a question, compare how you work, and talk project controls, project finance, governed AI and careers with peers around the world. No account needed: pick a display name and join in.

Community guidelines — please read before posting
  • Be respectful and professional — debate ideas, never people.
  • No unlawful, infringing, offensive or confidential material.
  • Never share employer-confidential data, figures or documents.
  • Never solicit or disclose examination content — doing so is a conduct matter under PCI's certification policies.
  • Posts are public and moderated. The Institute may hide or remove content at its discretion and will cooperate with lawful requests from authorities.
  • No personal data is collected beyond the display name you choose — do not post your own or anyone else's personal details.

Project Controls Institute Global, Inc. is a Delaware Non-Stock Corporation. It intends to seek recognition as a tax-exempt nonprofit organisation under Section 501(c)(3) of the U.S. Internal Revenue Code; tax-exempt status has not yet been granted, and contributions are not currently represented as tax-deductible. PCI is not currently accredited by ANAB, IAS, or any ISO/IEC 17024 accreditation body — its certification framework is being developed with reference to ISO/IEC 17024 personnel-certification principles. PCI credentials can be verified at any time through the public credential lookup. The PCI certifications are not government-recognised, ISO-accredited or formally accredited. Recognition and acceptance of PCI credentials may vary by employer, industry, institution and jurisdiction. PCI does not guarantee employment, promotion, salary improvement, immigration benefits, licensing eligibility or acceptance by any third party.
