Before you begin

What to have ready

The form takes a few minutes if you gather what it asks for first. Because your progress is saved against your email address, the most important decision is which address you use.

  • An email you will keep. Your saved progress, setup link and student panel access are all tied to it. Prefer a personal address over a work one you would lose on changing employer.
  • Your professional details. Current role, employer or institution, sector, years of experience and your main controls area. These shape the pathway suggested to you, and you can update them later.
  • A clear reason for joining. Whether you are preparing for the PCP-AI, meeting an employer requirement or exploring the discipline, answering honestly helps us point you at the right starting material.

If you are still weighing your options, read the student membership page first. Membership and certification are distinct steps, and the form will ask you to confirm you understand that.

After payment

Common questions about finishing enrolment

What happens after I pay?

You receive a secure setup link at the email you enrolled with. Follow it to set your own password, then sign in at the student login to reach your panel. For security, PCI never emails a plain-text password.

My setup link has not arrived — what should I do?

Check your spam or junk folder first, and make sure you are looking in the inbox for the exact address you entered on the form. If it still has not appeared, write to us from that same address and we will re-issue it promptly.

Can I change my selection after paying?

Changes and cancellations are handled under the published refund policy. Contact us with your enrolment email and what you would like to change, and we will set out your options plainly.
