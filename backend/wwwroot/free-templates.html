Student communication

What we’ll email you

Each step of your journey is confirmed by a clear, separate email — so a payment receipt, your secure account-setup link and your welcome never arrive jumbled into one message.

1

Payment confirmation

Sent the moment your payment is received — your receipt with item, amount and reference.

2

Account setup / login

A separate, secure link to set your password. For your security we never send a password by email.

3

Welcome

Your welcome to the PCI student community, your first steps and a link to your dashboard.

4

Exam confirmation

When you pay your PCI exam fee, confirmation that you may schedule your exam within 12 months.

5

Enrolment reminders

If you start but don’t finish, a friendly reminder a few hours later — then a couple more, so you can pick up where you left off.

6

Enquiry replies

A tailored acknowledgement for general, information, corporate / bulk and partnership enquiries.

Exam scheduling: once your PCL-AI examination fee is paid, you may schedule your exam at any time within 12 months of payment. Your confirmation email states your exact schedule-by date. If the window lapses, re-scheduling may require a new exam registration.

Where to start

A sensible reading order

Nobody should have to read every policy before acting. Match your reading to the step you are actually taking:

Before you enrol

Read the Terms of Enrolment and the Refund & Cancellation Policy — they define what enrolment provides and your options if plans change. Five minutes here prevents most later surprises.

Before you book an exam

The Candidate Handbook is the single best end-to-end read, backed by the Examination Rules and Eligibility Requirements. Note the 12-month scheduling window described above.

After you certify

Shift to the CPD Framework and Recertification policy — the documents that govern keeping your credential current rather than earning it.

Document control

How these documents are governed

Policies on this page are living documents in a founding-phase programme, so it matters to know which version counts. The version published on this site is the current one, and policies are reviewed and updated as the programme develops. Where a summary — on this page, in an email, or anywhere else — differs from the full published policy, the full policy prevails. Material changes to documents that affect candidates are flagged rather than made silently, in keeping with PCI's commitment to describing its status honestly at every stage. If you have relied on a document for a decision, keep a note of when you read it; if anything seems to have changed under you, ask via the contact page before assuming the worst. The complete library, beyond the highlights grouped above, lives at all policies & documents.

Email safety

Recognising genuine PCI email

Because each step of your journey triggers its own email, it is worth knowing what genuine PCI messages never do:

  • They never ask you to reply with your password, and never contain one — account links are for setting a password, not confirming it
  • They never demand urgent payment through a link in the email itself
  • They arrive one step at a time by design: a receipt, an account-setup link and a welcome are separate messages, so a single email claiming to be all three is suspect

If a message feels wrong, do not click anything in it. Sign in by typing the site address yourself, or verify the message before acting. Reporting a suspicious email helps protect other candidates too.
