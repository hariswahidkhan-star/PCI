# Domain 1 — Foundations of Accounting · Lecture

A premium, animated lecture for Domain 1 of the PCL-AI programme, built the way the evidence says people
learn best from lecture video: short learner-paced segments, every concept dual-coded with a visual,
signalling on the key idea, worked examples, and a knowledge check per Knowledge Area (Mayer's multimedia
principles / cognitive-load theory; Guo et al., *How Video Production Affects Student Engagement*, 2014).

## What's here

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `domain-01-foundations.html` | The **animated deck** — self-contained (no external assets). Open in a browser. |
| `Domain-01-Foundations-slides.pdf` | The 28 slides as a **static PDF** (final build state of each). |
| `Domain-01-Foundations-narration-script.md` / `.pdf` | The **word-for-word narration** for recording the video lecture (~50–55 min). |

## Using the deck

- **Advance a build:** `→` or `Space` · **Back:** `←` · **Jump to a KA:** number keys `1`–`5`
- **Speaker notes:** `N` (shows the narration in the caption bar) · **Auto-play:** `P` · **Light/dark:** `T`
- Click the left/right third of the screen to go back/forward; click a rail segment to jump.

## Coverage (all 5 Knowledge Areas)

KA 1.1 the accounting model (equation, debits/credits, journal→ledger→trial balance, its limits) ·
KA 1.2 the four financial statements and how they articulate · KA 1.3 accrual accounting & matching ·
KA 1.4 provisions & the IAS 37 recognition test · KA 1.5 chart of accounts & cost coding onto the WBS.

The running worked example is **Meridian Rail Contracting LLC**, consistent with the Body of Knowledge.
