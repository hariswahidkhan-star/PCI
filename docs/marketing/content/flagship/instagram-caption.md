# Nobody was careless. The CPI moved fourteen points.

## The caption

*Post exactly as written. Line breaks matter: Instagram collapses the caption after roughly 125 characters and the first line is doing the whole job.*

> Nobody was careless. The CPI still moved fourteen points. Slide 3 is the arithmetic, in four lines.
>
> Earned value 2,200,000. Invoiced cost 1,850,000. That is a CPI of 1.19, and a cost report that says the job is running under budget.
>
> Then somebody remembers the 240,000 of work that is done and not yet invoiced. Cost becomes 2,090,000.
>
> 2,200,000 ÷ 2,090,000 = 1.05.
>
> Work done and not yet invoiced sits in neither system. Earned value has already counted it. The ledger has not seen it. It belongs to nobody until somebody goes and asks for it.
>
> The error is accounting. The damage is delivery. Neither training alone catches it.
>
> Accountancy exams rarely test float. Engineering exams rarely test cut-off. The gap is in what each profession is examined on, never in the people doing the work.
>
> That gap is what the PCI AI Project Controls Leader (PCL-AI) exists to examine: 13 domains, 61 knowledge areas, both ledgers in one examination. Its Body of Knowledge runs 40 per cent finance and reporting, 40 project management, 20 governed AI.
>
> Our own arithmetic is checked too. 15,613 machine calculation checks run against the PCI AI Project Finance Leader (PFL-AI) and the PCI Project Management Leader – AI (PML-AI), all passing.
>
> Read the domains. Hold them against your own last month-end. Link in bio.
>

*Alt text: Three lines of white text on black. A CPI of 1.19 against a CPI of 1.05, and the question of which one reaches the board.*

**On slide:**

> Invoiced cost: 1,850,000.

*Design: the two figures stacked as a mock report line, ruled underneath. The last line in a lighter weight, set apart. 23 words.*

### Slide 3

**On slide:**

> 2,200,000 ÷ 1,850,000 = 1.19
> 1,850,000 + 240,000 = 2,090,000
> 2,200,000 ÷ 2,090,000 = 1.05
> Fourteen points. One missing accrual.

*Design: this is the screenshot slide. Four lines, monospaced, generous leading, one accent colour on the two results and nothing else in colour. No logo, no watermark, no handle across the numbers. Anything added here is something a reader has to crop out before they can share it. 20 words.*
*Alt text: Four lines of arithmetic. Earned value divided by invoiced cost gives 1.19. Adding a 240,000 accrual to cost gives 2,090,000, and the same earned value divided by that gives 1.05.*
