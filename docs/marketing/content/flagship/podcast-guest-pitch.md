# Guest: the accrual that moved CPI fourteen points

*Podcast guest pitch. Everything below the rule is the message exactly as it is sent: plain text, one named host, no attachment.*

---

**Subject:** Guest: the accrual that moved CPI fourteen points

[First name],

Your CPI reads 1.19. The missing accrual says 1.05. Which number reaches the board?

Earned value 2,200,000, invoiced cost 1,850,000. CPI 1.19, so the job reads under budget. Add 240,000 of work done and not yet invoiced: cost 2,090,000, CPI 1.05. Fourteen points moved and nothing in the schedule changed. Illustrative arithmetic, not a case study. It runs in ninety seconds and your listeners can follow it without a slide.

Three episodes. Take one, or none.

1. **"The forecast was wrong before you signed it."** Cut-off, accruals, and the number you defended to people who could overrule you.
2. **"Two exams, one project, nothing in the middle."** Accountancy exams rarely test float. Engineering exams rarely test cut-off. Projects need both.
3. **"Who signs the forecast when the model wrote it?"** Governed AI as an accountability question, not a tooling one.

Why me: I wrote the PCI AI Project Controls Leader (PCL-AI) Body of Knowledge, 13 domains and 61 knowledge areas, on 113 mandatory PCI Standards and 532 process requirements.

And ask me the hostile one on air. Who accredits you: nobody. I would rather answer that in your first five minutes than leave it under the whole episode.

Forty-five minutes, remote, any week you have a gap. The syllabus is published in full if you would rather read it first. If it is no, no reply is needed.

[SENDER NAME]
[TITLE], Project Controls Institute Global
https://projectcontrolsinstitute.org/body-of-knowledge
