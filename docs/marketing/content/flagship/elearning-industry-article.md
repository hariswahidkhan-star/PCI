# What Makes A Credential Examinable: An L&D Buyer's Test

What makes a credential examinable is not the subject matter. It is the grammar of the claims. A credential is examinable when every claim it makes names something a candidate can be observed doing, under stated conditions, with an answer two markers who have never met would mark the same way.

A syllabus that fails that test is not a weak credential. It is a reading list with a certificate at the end.

A profession is shaped by what it is examined on, not by what it is taught. Accountancy examinations rarely test float. Engineering examinations rarely test cut-off. Both are examined well on their own side of a line every project is assembled across.

## What Makes A Credential Examinable?

Most syllabus lines are written as aims: "understands", "is familiar with", "appreciates the importance of". None of those is a thing a person does, so none can be marked, and none can be failed.

Rewrite the line as a claim about behaviour and the question writes itself.

| Written as an aim | Written as an examinable claim | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| Understands revenue recognition | Determines, at a stated cut-off, whether performance to date gives a contract asset or a receivable | A moment and a decision, so something can be wrong |
| Is familiar with earned value management | Recomputes the cost performance index after an accrual for work performed and not yet invoiced | An operation on given figures, with one defensible result |
| Appreciates the role of AI in forecasting | States which forecast decisions a model may inform and which it may not take | A boundary, which can be drawn in the wrong place |
| Knows the four estimate at completion methods | Selects a method and names the assumption that would invalidate it | The assumption is the competence. The formula is on a card |

The third row is a policy question before it is technical: [which forecast decisions a model may originate and which it may only restate](https://pciai.org/ai-policy-for-project-controls) is settled in advance, or discovered late.

The second row, in full. Earned value is 2,200,000. Invoiced cost is 1,850,000. Divide one by the other and the cost performance index reads 1.19, so the report says the job is running under budget. But 240,000 of work is done and not yet invoiced. Accrue it, and cost becomes 2,090,000. Divide again: 2,200,000 ÷ 2,090,000 = 1.05.

Fourteen points moved on one journal entry, and nothing happened on site. The error is accounting. The damage is delivery. Neither training alone catches it.

A candidate either raises that accrual or does not. There is no partial credit for awareness, which is nearly the whole of what examinable means.

## Why Is A Syllabus Without A Compliance Test Only An Aspiration?

Everybody tests the candidate. Almost nobody tests the syllabus.

A compliance test is the check that runs against the document rather than the person: whether its arithmetic computes, whether every claim traces to a requirement, and whether two clauses can both be satisfied. It runs before the first candidate sits, and catches three expensive things.

- **A worked example that does not reconcile.** It becomes a question nobody can defend on appeal, which is how you find out.
- **A claim with nothing behind it.** Where a syllabus line traces to no requirement, the marker invents the standard while marking, and it differs by marker.
- **Two requirements that cannot both be met.** Candidates find these faster than authors do, and they find them in the exam hall.

Until that check has been run, a syllabus is a statement of intent. So ask what the test found, how many checks it ran and what they covered. The count and its scope are a fact about the document; their absence is a fact about the provider.

## What Should You Ask Before You Fund A Cohort?

Four questions. They take ten minutes and work on any scheme in any discipline.

1. **Show me a claim a candidate could fail.** If every line begins "understands", the assessment cannot produce a bad result, and one that cannot produce a bad result cannot produce information.
2. **What is the pass decision made against?** A fixed standard somebody can point at, or the shape of this cohort's marks? One tells you about the person, the other about the group they sat with.
3. **Who tested the syllabus, and what did they find?** Not the candidates. The document. Ask for the number of checks and their scope, and be wary of a number quoted without one.
4. **What have you not decided yet?** Every scheme in development has open decisions. A body that names one is telling you where it stands. A body with none has finished, or is selling.

## What It Costs To Get This Wrong

You fund a cohort. Everybody passes. The feedback scores are good, and a forecast later reaches a board with fourteen points of error in it, because nothing in the programme required anyone to raise an accrual.

That failure is invisible by design. A programme that cannot fail anyone cannot tell you anything about the people who passed it.

So do the cheap version first. Take one line from the syllabus you are being sold and ask what a candidate must do to fail it. If nobody can answer, the certificate will tell you nothing you did not already know.

Then [read the 13 domains and 61 knowledge areas of PCL-AI in full](https://projectcontrolsinstitute.org/body-of-knowledge) and run the four questions over those too. If a line will not survive the first one, we would rather hear it from you.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the difference between a syllabus and an examination?**

A syllabus is a promise about what will be covered. An examination is a promise about what must be true of the person holding the certificate. The first can be satisfied by delivering material to an empty room. The second cannot.

**What makes a learning objective examinable?**

An examinable objective names an action, the conditions it is performed under, and what counts as getting it wrong. "Understands cut-off" is not examinable. "Determines whether an accrual is required for work performed before the ledger closed" is.

**Can a credential be examinable without a written examination?**

Yes. A portfolio, a viva or an observed assessment is examinable if it produces a defensible pass or fail against a stated standard, marked consistently by separate assessors. The test is whether a competent assessor could say no, say why, and say the same again next Tuesday.

**Does a body of knowledge weighting tell me the examination weighting?**

No, and treating one as the other is the commonest error in this market. A body of knowledge weighting describes how the material is proportioned. An examination weighting describes how the paper is built, and that decision is often open. Ask which you are being quoted.
