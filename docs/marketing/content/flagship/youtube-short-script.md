## Production notes

**Shot.** Text on black throughout, or a talking head for beats 1, 6 and 7 only with full-frame text for 2 to 5 and 8. Do not cut to a face on beat 4. A face on the arithmetic frame halves the screenshot rate and adds nothing.

**Safe zones.** Keep every word inside a centre band: 240 px clear at the top, 420 px clear at the bottom, 220 px clear on the right. TikTok's caption block and Reels' account row both eat the bottom of the frame, and the arithmetic on beat 4 is the one thing that must never be sitting under a username.

**Captions.** Burned in, one to two lines, positioned above the bottom safe margin. Upload a clean SRT to YouTube as well, because the transcript is what a language model reads. Check the transcript for "CPI", "accrual" and "cut-off" before publishing.

**Sound.** A low bed under beats 1 to 3, out entirely across beat 4, back under 6 to 8. No riser, no whoosh on the number reveals. The silence on the pause frame is doing the work.

**The 45-second trim,** if the read runs slow: cut "Nobody has done anything wrong yet" from beat 2 and "And nobody was careless" from beat 6, and shorten the beat 4 hold from three seconds to two. Nothing else may come out. The three lines of arithmetic, the 240,000 in the gap, and the two-column exam split are the piece.

**Never cut this down to a quote card.** The arithmetic is the reason it travels, and a still of beat 6 without beats 2 to 4 is an opinion.
