# Cost side rather than planning side, here to be useful

Hi all. Cost side rather than planning side. Here to be useful, not to post links.

The thing I keep going on about:

```
Earned value    2,200,000
Invoiced cost   1,850,000
2,200,000 ÷ 1,850,000 = CPI 1.19
```

Under budget. That goes to the board. Then someone asks what is done and not yet invoiced.

```
Work done, not yet invoiced: 240,000
1,850,000 + 240,000 = 2,090,000
2,200,000 ÷ 2,090,000 = CPI 1.05
```

Fourteen points, one accrual. Nothing overspent, nothing slipped. Illustrative round numbers, not a real job.

Nobody made a mistake. Two people each answered their own question correctly and the number between them was still wrong. Finance is examined on when a cost may be recognised, planners on float and the earning rule. CPI is a ratio of the two and neither exam covers both ends.

What I bring: month-end questions, any time, in whatever channel fits. Accruals, whether your actuals column holds committed, invoiced or incurred cost, cut-off against data date, why CPI moved when nothing did. I am slow, and I always show the arithmetic.

Interest declared: I work with the Project Controls Institute, an independent certifying body. The credential I work on, PCI AI Project Controls Leader (PCL-AI), examines 13 domains and 61 knowledge areas. It is in my profile. Read it against your own last close. I will not paste it in channels.

One thing back: has anyone here seen a CPI reported with its cost basis written underneath? I have not, and would like to be wrong.
