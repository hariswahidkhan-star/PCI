The most dangerous number on a project is a good one.

Earned value 2,200,000. Invoiced cost 1,850,000. CPI 1.19, under budget. Then someone asks what's done and not invoiced: 240,000. Cost is 2,090,000. CPI 1.05.

Fourteen points, one missing accrual. The error is accounting. The damage is delivery. Neither profession is examined on the other's half.
