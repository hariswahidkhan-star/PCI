**2/**

Earned value: 2,200,000.
Invoiced cost: 1,850,000.

2,200,000 ÷ 1,850,000 = 1.19

CPI above 1. Under budget. Green on the dashboard.

Nobody has done anything wrong yet. The cost engineer reported exactly what the ledger gave them.

---

**3/**

Then someone asks what has been done and not yet invoiced.

240,000.

1,850,000 + 240,000 = 2,090,000
2,200,000 ÷ 2,090,000 = 1.05

Fourteen CPI points, moved by an invoice that has not arrived yet.
