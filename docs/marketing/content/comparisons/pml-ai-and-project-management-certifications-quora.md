# Project management certification, or PML-AI instead?

Sit a project management certification if you are building or proving the delivery discipline: scope, stakeholders, schedule, cost, quality, risk, procurement and leading the people doing the work. Sit the PCI Project Management Leader – AI (PML-AI) if you are already accountable for what a project reports and for what a model is allowed to decide inside it.

**Disclosure first:** credentialfinder.org, where the longer comparison sits, is operated by Project Controls Institute Global, which awards the PCL-AI, PFL-AI and PML-AI credentials mentioned here. Treat this as an interested party writing carefully.

## What each one is examined on

Project management certifications examine managing projects across people, process and the business environment. That is a large body of knowledge and these certifications go a long way into it. A holder can set a project up, run it, and keep it honest against a plan.

PML-AI covers 16 domains and 63 knowledge areas on a Body of Knowledge weighted 40 / 40 / 20 across finance and reporting, project management and governed AI. Those are the proportions of the syllabus, not of any examination. In practice that means the delivery discipline, plus where the project's numbers land in the accounts, plus what a model may and may not decide.

## The part most managers have never been examined on

Take the AI half, since it is the newest. A schedule risk model flags 120 activities in a quarter and 78 of them slip, so precision is 78 ÷ 120 = **0.65**. Of the 130 activities that actually slipped, 78 were flagged, so recall is 78 ÷ 130 = **0.60**. The combined measure, F1, is 2 × (0.65 × 0.60) ÷ 1.25 = **0.624**.

Read plainly: 42 flags were false and 52 slips arrived with no warning. That makes the model a good way to order your attention and a poor basis for closing a risk.

The governance rule that follows is short. A model may propose. A named person decides. The record shows who decided, on what basis and when. If you could not reconstruct that a year later, the tool is running the project.

## How to choose

Take a project management certification if your employer names one, if you want the established route into project delivery, or if you need an accredited credential now. A mandate is not a comparison, and that last point is a complete answer on its own.

Take PML-AI if you are the person asked why the forecast moved and what it does to the reported result, or if you sign off model-assisted outputs and want the reasoning examined rather than assumed.

Doing both, delivery discipline first, is a sensible order mid-career. They are not competing subjects.

PCI is a new, independent certifying body. It is not accredited by ANAB, UKAS, IAS or any other ISO/IEC 17024 accreditation body, and does not claim to be. The scheme is built with reference to ISO/IEC 17024 principles. The whole Body of Knowledge is published before anyone pays anything, so read it and judge the scope yourself: https://projectcontrolsinstitute.org/body-of-knowledge
