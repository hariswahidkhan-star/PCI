# 19% ahead or 6% behind: why syllabus depth decides

*X / Threads thread — 7 posts, each under 280 characters and each able to stand alone. No link in the thread; the link sits in the first reply. X counts a URL as 23 characters.*

**Post 1/7 — the hook** (204 characters)
A syllabus that lists topics and nothing else cannot be judged. It can only be admired.

What it should tell you: what is examined, at what depth, in what proportion, and what it assumes you already know.

**Post 2/7 — the check people skip** (198 characters)
Depth is the check people skip, and it is where the preparation hours actually go.

"Cost performance index" on a syllabus line could mean recall. It could mean judgement. Those are different exams.

**Post 3/7 — the arithmetic** (219 characters)
At cut-off: £2.35m of supplier invoices posted, £0.64m received but not yet invoiced. Earned value £2.80m.

On posted cost: 2.80 ÷ 2.35 = 1.191
With the accrual: 2.80 ÷ 2.99 = 0.937

19% ahead, or 6% behind. Same month.

**Post 4/7 — where the number came from** (236 characters)
Nothing on site changed between those two numbers.

A cut-off convention that lives on the finance side of the house landed on the delivery side's index. That join is where the money goes, and it belongs to nobody's syllabus by default.

**Post 5/7 — recall against judgement** (212 characters)
Recall depth: you can define the index.

Judgement depth: you can say which figure goes in the board pack, what you attach to it, and what you do about the £0.64m next month.

Read the verbs, not the topic count.

**Post 6/7 — the two checks nobody runs** (226 characters)
Two more checks while the document is open.

What does it assume you already know? Candidates rarely fail on topics. They fail on situations.

What does it explicitly exclude? Every unstated boundary becomes an argument later.

**Post 7/7 — the disclosure** (270 characters)
If your employer or a client's pre-qualification names a certification, take the named one. A scope argument does not beat a procurement condition.
