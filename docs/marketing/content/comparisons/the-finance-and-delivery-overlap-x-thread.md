# The finance and delivery overlap, in nine short posts

*X / Threads thread — 9 posts, each under 280 characters and each able to stand alone. The link goes in the first reply. Character counts are for production; X counts any URL as 23 characters.*

**Post 1/9 — the hook** (190 characters)
A delivery team revised an estimate for delivery reasons. Reported margin fell by £0.57m in the same month, on the same work in the ground. Nobody did anything wrong. The arithmetic follows.

**Post 2/9 — the base case** (165 characters)
Contract price £26.0m. Costs incurred £9.0m. Estimated total cost £24.0m.

Progress = 9.0 ÷ 24.0 = 37.5%
Revenue = 0.375 × 26.0 = £9.75m
Margin = 9.75 − 9.0 = £0.75m

**Post 3/9 — the revision** (190 characters)
Now the estimate moves to £25.5m. A subcontractor replaced, a rate agreed, three weeks of weather.

Progress = 9.0 ÷ 25.5 = 35.3%
Revenue = 0.353 × 26.0 = £9.18m
Margin = 9.18 − 9.0 = £0.18m

**Post 4/9 — what actually happened** (202 characters)
The denominator in that fraction is a forecast delivery owns. The output is revenue finance reports. A delivery judgement became a reported result through a mechanism neither profession was examined on.

**Post 5/9 — two of the four artefacts** (217 characters)
Four artefacts live in that join. The cut-off calendar: progress to one date, costs to another, an index that moves for reasons that are not performance. The accrual list: work done, not yet invoiced, quietly missing.

**Post 6/9 — the other two** (226 characters)
The earning rule: 50/50 and 0/100 on the same activity give different earned value in the same month. The change register: instructed not priced, priced not approved, approved not in the budget. Three states, three treatments.

**Post 7/9 — who examines what** (223 characters)
Cost and scheduling credentials examine estimating, cost control, planning and schedule analysis. Accountancy qualifications examine recognition, measurement, disclosure and control. Each goes at its own subject thoroughly.

**Post 8/9 — when this is not your problem** (189 characters)
If your work stops at the programme, the join is somebody else's week and a scheduling credential examines what you actually do. The overlap matters when you sign something that crosses it.
