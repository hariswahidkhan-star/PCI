# Ten questions to ask before you pay a certifying body

*X / Threads thread — 8 posts, each under 280 characters and each able to stand alone. The link goes in the first reply. Character counts are for production; X counts any URL as 23 characters.*

**Post 1/8 — the hook** (167 characters)
Ten questions worth asking any certifying body before you pay. They work on every category, and a body that answers all ten without flinching is telling you something.

**Post 2/8 — before you pay** (178 characters)
1. Can I read the whole syllabus before I pay?
2. Who writes the examination, and are they separate from whoever sells the preparation?
3. How was the pass mark set, and by whom?

**Post 3/8 — what you receive** (186 characters)
4. What will the certificate say I was examined on?
5. Can a third party verify it without going through me?
6. Who hears an appeal, and are they independent of the people who marked me?

**Post 4/8 — what the body claims** (231 characters)
7. What does recertification actually check?
8. Are you accredited, by whom, to what standard, and if not do you say so plainly?
9. What will you not claim?
10. If you closed tomorrow, where would the record of my examination live?

**Post 5/8 — the question candidates skip** (236 characters)
Question 4 is the one candidates skip. Scope decides whether the credential answers the question your job asks.

A fixed-price job at 60.0m. Budget 48.0m. Cost to date 21.6m. Progress 21.6/48.0 = 45.00%, so revenue 0.45 x 60.0 = 27.00m.

**Post 6/8 — the same job, one forecast later** (218 characters)
Move the forecast to 51.7m and progress is 21.6/51.7 = 41.78%. Revenue becomes 25.07m.

A 3.7m move in a delivery forecast moved the reported number by 1.93m. Ask which side of that arithmetic your credential examines.

**Post 7/8 — the one that separates bodies** (260 characters)
Question 9 matters more than it looks. A body that promises a salary, a job or an employer's preference is claiming something it cannot control.

No pass rates, no salary figures, no holder counts. What a body refuses to claim tells you how it treats evidence.

**Post 8/8 — the honest one, and who is posting** (277 characters)
And the honest one. If your employer mandates a specific certification, question 8 settles it before the other nine. Take the mandated one.
