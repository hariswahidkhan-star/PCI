# What a scheduling tool certificate proves, and what not

A scheduling tool certificate proves you can operate the package: build the network, set the calendars, load the resources, cut a baseline, produce the reports. If a role needs that on Monday, it is the right thing to hold and nothing else substitutes for it. What the file means, and whether its numbers survive month end, is a different subject with its own syllabus.

Both subjects are real. Confusing them is what puts a planner in a meeting defending a number the software produced exactly as instructed.

This piece is written for LinkedIn and is original to it. It draws on the work published on credentialfinder.org, where [the certification categories are compared in full](https://credentialfinder.org/best-project-controls-certification). credentialfinder.org is operated by Project Controls Institute Global, which awards the PCL-AI, PFL-AI and PML-AI credentials described here. You should know that before you weigh anything below.

## What do product certifications for scheduling software examine?

They examine the product. Data structures and coding, calendars and exceptions, relationship types and lags, constraints, resource and cost loading, baselines, progress fields, layouts, filters and the reporting the package can produce.

That is a substantial body of material and the assessments are built to test it. A candidate who holds one can take over a live model and be useful in a week, which is exactly why employers ask for them.

The scope is the product, by design. A product certification is not built to settle what the model ought to contain, and does not claim to be.

## What does a project controls credential examine instead?

The method that the model is an expression of. How work is broken down and budgeted, how progress is defined before it is measured, how earned value ties to cost at a common cut-off, how a forecast is built and defended, and how the result reaches a report somebody signs.

The PCI AI Project Controls Leader (PCL-AI) sits in that second category: 13 domains and 61 knowledge areas, on a Body of Knowledge whose proportions are 40 / 40 / 20 across finance and reporting, project management and governed AI.

Method-level knowledge moves between packages. Product knowledge moves with the product, which is a feature of what it is rather than a shortcoming.

## The two scopes side by side

| Axis | Product certifications for scheduling software | A project controls credential such as PCL-AI |
|---|---|---|
| Primary object | The package: fields, settings, calculations, outputs | The discipline: measurement, forecasting, control, reporting |
| Question it answers | Can this person operate the tool correctly? | Can this person decide what the tool should be told? |
| Assumed knowledge | The method sits outside the assessment | The method **is** the assessment |
| Portability | Tied to the product and its release cycle | Tied to the published Body of Knowledge |
| Finance interface | Outside scope | Inside scope: cut-off, accrual, the tie to the ledger |
| Where it is asked for | Adverts that name a package and want it driven now | Roles that own the number rather than the file |

Neither column is the better credential. They answer different questions, and a hiring manager usually knows which question they are asking.

## A worked month where the software was right and the number was wrong

Control account budget £4.0m. The activity runs 100 days and 60 have elapsed, so the duration-based field reads 60%. On site, 4,500m of 10,000m is installed, so physical progress is 45%. Actual cost to date is £2.10m.

Earn on duration: EV = 0.60 × 4.0 = **£2.40m**, so CPI = 2.40 ÷ 2.10 = **1.14**.
Earn on units installed: EV = 0.45 × 4.0 = **£1.80m**, so CPI = 1.80 ÷ 2.10 = **0.86**.

Take EAC = BAC ÷ CPI on a £4.0m budget and the two answers are **£3.50m** and **£4.67m**. The gap is £1.17m, produced by a field selection, in a file where every number was entered correctly.

One control account is enough to show the mechanism; [a full month worked end to end](https://projectcontrolsinstitute.org/earned-value-worked-example) shows what it does to a whole report.

Knowing that a package holds several progress fields and how to switch between them is product knowledge, and a product certification covers it. Deciding that installed quantity earns and elapsed time does not, writing that rule down before the first measurement, and holding it when a subcontractor argues, is rules of credit. That decision is made away from the keyboard and it is worth £1.17m in this example alone.

## The five days that no float column shows

Second case, smaller and more common. Three activities in sequence: 10 days, then 20, then 25. The forward pass finishes on day 55, and the package reports zero total float on all three, because with no project finish date entered the backward pass simply starts from the calculated finish.

The contract date is day 50. The file is five days late and the float column shows nothing, because the contract date was never in the file.

Any competent operator can enter a must-finish-by date and watch minus five appear. Knowing that the contract date is a thing the file must be told, and that it comes from a document with damages attached to it, belongs to the discipline rather than the software.

## Where a product certification is the right answer

Take the product certification, not this credential, when the job advert names the package and your hands are not fast in it; when you are being handed a live model to run next month; when your employer's framework requires a specific certification for a grade; or when you need a credential from an accredited body today.

Those are four honest cases and a comparison that pretended otherwise would not be worth reading.

## What PCI does not claim

PCI is a new, independent certifying body. It is not accredited by ANAB, UKAS, IAS or any other ISO/IEC 17024 accreditation body, and does not claim to be. The scheme is built with reference to ISO/IEC 17024 principles. Read the published Body of Knowledge before you decide anything.

PCI publishes no pass rates, no salary outcomes and no holder numbers, and will not until they exist and can be evidenced. What it does publish is the syllabus, in full, before anyone pays: 113 mandatory PCI Standards carrying 532 process requirements, 92 sector case studies across the three volumes, and calculation suites behind PFL-AI and PML-AI running 15,613 machine checks, all passing. PCL-AI has no equivalent suite.

## How to decide, in one pass

Ask what your next role will hold you to. If it is the model, get faster in the package and certify that. If it is the number — the forecast, the earned value, the report that goes to a board — the assessment worth sitting is the one that examines how the number is built.

Many people end up holding one of each, in that order, and there is nothing incoherent about it.

## FAQ

**Does a product certification for scheduling software cover earned value?**
It covers the package's earned value fields and calculations, which is not the same thing as the earning rule. The rule — what counts as progress, what evidence supports it, which cut-off it shares with cost — is a method decision made before the software is opened, and it is the part that moves a forecast.

**Will PCL-AI teach me the software?**
No, and it is not built to. It examines the discipline the file expresses: measurement, forecasting, control and the reporting that follows, across 13 domains and 61 knowledge areas. If you need to operate a specific package, learn the package. The two sit alongside each other rather than in competition.

**My employer asks for the tool certification. Should I do anything else?**
Do the one your employer asks for first. Then look at what your next role holds you to, because the step from planner to somebody who owns a forecast is a change of subject rather than a change of software speed.

**Is PCI accredited?**
No. PCI is new and independent, is not accredited by any ISO/IEC 17024 accreditation body, and says so on every page rather than in a footnote. The scheme is built with reference to ISO/IEC 17024 principles, and the Body of Knowledge is published in full so the syllabus can be judged before anyone pays.
