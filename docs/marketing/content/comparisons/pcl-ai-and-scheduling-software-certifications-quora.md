# Is a scheduling software certificate enough for controls?

Short answer: it is usually enough to get the first job, and it is not the thing you will be judged on in the third one.

A product certification for scheduling software examines operating the package — data structures and coding, calendars, relationships and lags, constraints, resource and cost loading, baselines, progress fields, layouts and reporting. That material is real and the assessments test it properly. It is also exactly what a hiring manager wants when a live model needs someone competent on it next month. If an advert names a package and your hands are not fast in it, do that certification first. Nothing substitutes for it.

What sits outside it, by design, is what the model should contain.

Here is the cleanest illustration I know, and you can check the arithmetic in your head.

Control account budget £4.0m. Actual cost to date £2.10m. The activity runs 100 days and 60 have elapsed, so the duration-based progress field reads 60%. On site, 4,500m of 10,000m is installed, so physical progress is 45%.

Earn on duration: EV = 0.60 × 4.0 = £2.40m. CPI = 2.40 ÷ 2.10 = 1.14.
Earn on units installed: EV = 0.45 × 4.0 = £1.80m. CPI = 1.80 ÷ 2.10 = 0.86.

Using EAC = BAC ÷ CPI, the same £4.0m budget forecasts out at £3.50m or £4.67m. That is £1.17m of difference, in a file where nobody typed a wrong number. The software earned value against the field it was told to earn against, which is what software does.

Choosing which field earns is a rules-of-credit decision. It is written down before the first measurement, supported by evidence at each measurement, applied identically every period, and defended at month end against a ledger that closed on its own cut-off date. None of that happens inside the package.

The same pattern turns up in the schedule. Three activities in sequence — 10 days, then 20, then 25 — finish on day 55. With no project finish date entered, the backward pass starts from that calculated finish, so total float reads zero on all three. If the contract date is day 50, the programme is five days late and the float column shows nothing. Entering a must-finish-by date takes two minutes. Knowing the file has to be told the contract date, and where that date comes from, is the other subject.

So the practical route for most people is both, in order: get certified on the package your market runs, then get examined on the discipline once you are the person defending the number.
