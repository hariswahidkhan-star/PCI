# Finance qualifications and PFL-AI: the join compared

*LinkedIn document post — 10 slides, 1080 × 1350. No link in the body; the link goes in the first comment.*

**Post caption (the first two lines carry the post):**

A cost engineer moved a forecast by £3.4m on a Tuesday. £1.82m of margin that had already been reported came back out the same month.

Nobody spent a penny. Ten slides on the join between two kinds of credential, and which one you should actually sit.

---

**Slide 1 — Two syllabuses, one contract**
Accountancy and finance qualifications examine recognition, measurement, disclosure and financial control. Cost and scheduling credentials examine estimating, cost control, planning and schedule analysis. A contract is examined by both and owned by neither.

**Slide 2 — The moment they meet**
It meets on the day a cost engineer changes an estimate at completion. In a cost report that is a forecast. In the ledger, under a cost-to-cost measure of progress, it is the denominator of the revenue fraction.

**Slide 3 — The arithmetic**
Fixed price **£40.0m**. Expected cost **£34.0m**, so expected margin £6.0m. Costs incurred **£17.0m**.

Progress = 17.0 ÷ 34.0 = **50.00%**. Revenue = 0.5000 × 40.0 = **£20.00m**. Cost £17.0m. Margin **£3.00m**.

Forecast moves to **£37.4m**. Progress = 17.0 ÷ 37.4 = **45.45%**. Revenue = £18.18m. Cost is still £17.0m. Margin **£1.18m**.

Cross-check: expected margin is now 40.0 − 37.4 = £2.6m, and 45.45% of £2.6m is £1.18m. Two routes, one answer. **£1.82m reversed, no cash moved.**

**Slide 4 — Neither party made an error**
The cost engineer produced a better forecast. The accountant applied the measure correctly. The failure, when it happens, is that nobody in the chain was examined on the sentence that connects them.

**Slide 5 — What a finance qualification is built to examine**
When revenue may be recognised, how an asset or a provision is measured, what must be disclosed, and how financial control operates across an entity. That is a deep subject and these qualifications go deep into it.

**Slide 6 — What a delivery credential is built to examine**
Estimating, cost control, planning, schedule analysis and the economics of an asset over its life. Also a deep subject, also thoroughly examined. Cut-off and contract assets sit outside it by design, not by neglect.

**Slide 7 — The join, named**
Five things live in the join: the cut-off date, the evidence behind an accrual, the rule of credit that turns work into earned value, the forecast that sets recognised revenue, and the test that turns an expected overrun into a loss taken in full, now.

**Slide 8 — Where PFL-AI sits**
PCI AI Project Finance Leader (PFL-AI) examines that join, across 16 domains and 61 knowledge areas, with the Body of Knowledge published in full before anyone pays anything.

Disclosure: credentialfinder.org, where the full comparison sits, is operated by Project Controls Institute Global, which awards PFL-AI.

**Slide 9 — When the other route is the right one**
If you will prepare or sign statutory accounts, work in practice, or need a route toward a practising qualification, take the accountancy route and do not think twice. If your employer mandates a specific certification, that is not a debate either.
