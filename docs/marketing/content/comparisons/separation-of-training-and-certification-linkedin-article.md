# Who marks your exam should not be who sold the course

Who should mark your exam is a question with one structural answer: not the party that sold you the course. Preparation and the certification decision are kept apart for that reason alone. Buy training from whoever teaches it best. Sit the examination with a body that has no stake in your result. Where one organisation does both, ask how it keeps the two decisions apart, and expect a written answer.

credentialfinder.org is operated by Project Controls Institute Global, which awards the PCL-AI, PFL-AI and PML-AI credentials described here. Read that sentence as a reason to check everything below against the scheme's own published documents rather than take it from me.

## This is not an argument against training

Most people need preparation, and good preparation is worth paying for. Somebody who has run twenty month-end closes can teach you in a day what a syllabus takes a fortnight to describe.

The separation is not about whether courses are useful. It is about who signs the decision at the end, and what that person has riding on it.

A trainer's job is to get you ready. An examiner's job is to record accurately whether you are. Those are different jobs with different incentives, and when the same organisation holds both, the second one is the one under pressure.

## What separation looks like when it is real

| Check | Preparation and award separated | Preparation and award joined |
|---|---|---|
| The syllabus | Published in full, readable before you pay anything | Available in outline, the detail arrives with the course |
| Sitting the examination | Open to anyone who meets the published eligibility rules | A named course is required first |
| Claims about preparation | None about odds of passing | Its own route described as the one that prepares you properly |
| The decision | Made against the published syllabus, by people with no interest in which way it goes | Made by the organisation whose product is judged by the result |
| A disputed decision | Reviewed by somebody who did not make it | Reviewed inside the same commercial line |
| Impartiality | A published policy that names the conflicts and says what happens | Handled case by case, described on request |

Neither column is a description of anybody in particular. They are the two designs, and plenty of respected schemes sit somewhere between them and say so openly.

## The five checks, in the order worth running them

**1. Can you read the entire syllabus before paying?** Not a marketing outline. The domains, the knowledge areas, the depth expected in each. If the syllabus only exists inside the course, the course is the syllabus and you cannot judge either one first.

**2. Is a specific course required before you may sit?** A mandated course is a defensible design in a regulated trade where supervised practice is part of the competence. It is harder to defend where the same body sells the mandated course.

**3. Does the body claim its own preparation improves your odds?** This is the sentence to hunt for, and it is rarely stated as bluntly as "our course improves your chances". More often it appears as "official", or as a route described as the complete one.

**4. Who reviews a decision you disagree with?** The useful answer names a person or a panel that did not make the original decision. Anything vaguer is a promise to be reasonable, which is not a process.

**5. Is there a published impartiality policy?** It should name the conflicts the body actually has, including the commercial ones, and say what it does about each. A policy that only says the body is committed to fairness has told you nothing you could hold it to.

## Why an examinable item is what makes independence practical

Independence is a slogan until the questions have defensible answers. Take one and work it.

A package with a budget at completion of £24.0m. At the cut-off, earned value is £4.8m and actual cost, accruals included, is £5.6m.

- CPI = EV ÷ AC = 4.8 ÷ 5.6 = **0.857**
- EAC on the assumption that performance to date continues = BAC ÷ CPI = 24.0 ÷ 0.857 = **£28.0m**
- Variance at completion = 24.0 − 28.0 = **−£4.0m**

Check it in your head: the job is earning 86p of budgeted work for every £1 spent, so £24.0m of budgeted work will cost about £28.0m at that rate.

The answer is £28.0m, or it is wrong. It does not become £26m because the candidate attended a particular course, and no marker can be talked round on it. That is what an examinable subject gives a certifying body: the ability to be independent without being unkind, because the syllabus and the arithmetic do the arguing.

Where a subject cannot be examined that way, the body is grading judgement, and grading judgement is exactly where the identity of the marker starts to matter.

## What the separation costs the body doing it

Publishing a syllabus in full means competitors read it on the day it is released, and anyone may build a course against it without asking. That is the trade. PCI's Bodies of Knowledge are 13, 16 and 16 domains, and 61, 61 and 63 knowledge areas, across PCL-AI, PFL-AI and PML-AI, with 92 sector case studies and 113 PCI Standards carrying 532 process requirements. All of it can be read before anyone pays anything, which also means all of it can be copied.

It also removes the easiest revenue a certifying body has. Preparation sells to every candidate; an examination sells once. A body that does not sell the preparation gives up the larger half of the market it is standing in.

And it removes a promise the sales page would like to make. Nobody at PCI can tell you that a particular route will get you through, because nobody at PCI is selling one.

## Where this may not be your deciding factor

If your employer or your client mandates a specific certification, the mandate decides. Work out what is required, take that route, and treat everything in this article as background for the next decision rather than this one.

If you need a credential from an accredited scheme today — because a framework agreement, a tender rule or a regulator says so — take the accredited route. That is a real requirement and no amount of syllabus transparency substitutes for it.

If you are early in a career and what you need is somebody to teach you the discipline from the beginning, buy the teaching. Choose the certification separately, later, when you can read a syllabus and tell whether it describes work you recognise.

## FAQ

**Is it wrong for a certifying body to offer training at all?**
No. It is a design that carries a conflict, and the question is whether the body names the conflict and manages it. Published separation of the two decisions, a syllabus anyone can read, and a review route outside the commercial line are what managing it looks like in practice.

**How do I tell a credential from a certificate of attendance?**
A certificate of attendance records that you were there. A credential records that you were assessed against a published standard, by somebody with no stake in the outcome, and can be verified by a third party afterwards. Ask what the assessment was and who scored it.

**Does an independent examiner mean the exam is harder?**
Not necessarily, and difficulty is not the point. Independence changes who the decision belongs to, not where the standard sits. The standard is set by the syllabus and the pass mark process, both of which should be published.

**If PCI does not sell preparation, how am I supposed to prepare?**
Read the Body of Knowledge, work the sector case studies in it, and use whichever training you judge best — from anyone. PCI has no preferred provider and no course that improves your odds, because it has none.

**What should I ask a body before I pay?**
Send them one email: may I have the syllabus, the appeals policy and the impartiality policy before I apply. All three should already be public. How quickly and how completely that email is answered tells you most of what the policies would.
