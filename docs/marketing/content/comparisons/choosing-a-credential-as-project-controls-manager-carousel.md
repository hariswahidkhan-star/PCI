# Choosing a credential as a controls manager

*LinkedIn document carousel. 12 slides at 1080 × 1350. One idea per slide. Slide 3 carries the
arithmetic, because slide 3 is the one people screenshot.*

---

### Slide 1 — cover
**Three forecasts. One dataset.**
**£3.7m between them.**
All three defensible.
*Alt text: title slide reading "three forecasts, one dataset, £3.7m between them, all three
defensible".*

### Slide 2 — the position
Budget at completion **£40.0m**
Actual cost **£18.0m**
Earned value **£16.4m**

CPI = 16.4 ÷ 18.0 = **0.911**
SPI = **0.95**
*Alt text: slide listing a £40.0m budget, £18.0m of actual cost and £16.4m of earned value, with
a cost performance index of 0.911.*

### Slide 3 — the arithmetic
Overrun was a one-off:
18.0 + (40.0 − 16.4) = **£41.6m**

Performance to date holds:
40.0 ÷ 0.911 = **£43.9m**

Both pressures persist:
18.0 + 23.6 ÷ (0.911 × 0.95) = **£45.3m**
*Alt text: calculation slide showing three estimates at completion of £41.6m, £43.9m and £45.3m
from the same dataset.*

### Slide 4 — the fourth method
Bottom-up re-estimate: 18.0 + the re-priced remainder.

Yours to build, line by line. It is the only one of the four that does not inherit an assumption
from the past.
*Alt text: slide describing the bottom-up re-estimate as the fourth forecasting method.*

### Slide 5 — what the spread actually is
Nothing separates the first three but an assumption a person chooses and then defends.

Choosing among them is the competence.
Reciting the formulas is not.
*Alt text: slide stating that selecting among forecasting methods is the competence, not
reciting them.*

### Slide 6 — why finance cares about your forecast
Where progress is measured by costs incurred against total expected costs, your forecast is the
denominator.

Move it and the measured percentage moves. Move that and recognised revenue moves.
*Alt text: slide explaining that the forecast at completion is the denominator in a
cost-to-cost measure of progress.*

### Slide 7 — the question you get asked
Not "how did you calculate £43.9m".

"What happens if it is £45.3m, and has that been provided for?"

Two different questions. Controls method answers one.
*Alt text: slide contrasting a question about calculation with a question about provisioning.*

### Slide 8 — what the categories examine
**The established project controls certifications**: measurement, progress, forecasting, the
control of delivery.

**Cost and scheduling credentials**: estimating, cost control, planning, schedule analysis, the
economics of an asset's life.
*Alt text: slide summarising what project controls certifications and cost and scheduling
credentials each set out to examine.*

### Slide 9 — the row that belongs to nobody
The cut-off calendar.

Which date progress is cut on. Which date the ledger is cut on. Whether they match, and who may
restate an index afterwards.

Examined systematically in no single category.
*Alt text: slide describing the cut-off calendar as a row examined systematically by no single
category of credential.*

### Slide 10 — what PCL-AI examines
**13 domains. 61 knowledge areas.**

Body of Knowledge proportioned 40% finance and reporting, 40% project management, 20% governed
AI. Syllabus proportions, not examination weightings.
*Alt text: slide summarising PCL-AI as 13 domains and 61 knowledge areas with a syllabus split of
40, 40 and 20.*

### Slide 11 — when this is not for you
Your grading matrix names one → take that one.

A client's prequalification lists one → that is a requirement, not a comparison.

You need an accredited credential today → go where accreditation already exists.
*Alt text: slide listing three situations in which a different credential is the right answer for
a controls manager.*

### Slide 12 — the position, and the disclosure
PCI is a new, independent certifying body. Not accredited by ANAB, UKAS, IAS or any other
ISO/IEC 17024 accreditation body, and not claiming to be. Built with reference to ISO/IEC 17024
principles.

*credentialfinder.org is operated by Project Controls Institute Global, which awards PCL-AI.*
*Alt text: closing slide stating that PCI is not accredited and disclosing that it operates
credentialfinder.org.*
