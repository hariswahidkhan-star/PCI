# Driving the package, or owning the number: two jobs

*LinkedIn document post — 10 slides, 1080 × 1350. No link in the body; the link goes in the first comment.*

**Post caption (the first two lines carry the post):**

Nobody typed a wrong number, and the forecast moved £1.17m.
The package earned value against the field it was told to earn against, which is what packages do.

Ten slides on where a product certification for scheduling software stops and the discipline starts, with the arithmetic on slide 3.

---

**Slide 1 — Two jobs, often confused**
One job is to drive the planning package: build the network, set the calendars, load resources, cut a baseline, produce the reports. The other is to own the number the package produces, in front of people who will act on it. Different work, different assessments.

**Slide 2 — What a product certification examines**
Data structures and coding. Calendars and exceptions. Relationship types and lags. Constraints. Resource and cost loading. Baselines. Progress fields. Layouts, filters and reporting. Substantial material, tested seriously, and the reason employers ask for it: a holder can take over a live model and be useful inside a week.

**Slide 3 — The arithmetic**
Control account budget £4.0m. Actual cost to date £2.10m. The activity runs 100 days and 60 have elapsed, so the duration field reads 60%. On site, 4,500m of 10,000m is installed, so physical progress is 45%.

Earn on duration: EV = 0.60 × 4.0 = **£2.40m**. CPI = 2.40 ÷ 2.10 = **1.14**. EAC = BAC ÷ CPI = **£3.50m**.
Earn on units: EV = 0.45 × 4.0 = **£1.80m**. CPI = 1.80 ÷ 2.10 = **0.86**. EAC = **£4.67m**.

**£1.17m apart. Same file, same cut-off, every entry correct.**

**Slide 4 — The second case: five days nobody can see**
Three activities in sequence: 10, then 20, then 25 days. Forward pass lands on day 55. With no project finish date in the file, the backward pass starts from that same day, so total float reads zero on every activity. The contract date is day 50. The programme is five days late and the float column is silent.

**Slide 5 — Rules of credit are decided away from the keyboard**
What counts as progress, what evidence supports it, and which field carries it into earned value: written down before the first measurement, applied the same way every period, held when a subcontractor argues. That decision is worth more than any setting in the file.

**Slide 6 — One cut-off, two systems**
Progress is surveyed when the site can be walked. The ledger closes when the ledger closes. Earn to the same instant you spend, or the indices report a performance problem that never happened.

**Slide 7 — What travels, and what does not**
Product knowledge follows the product and its release cycle, which is what it is for. Method knowledge follows you between packages, employers and sectors. Most careers need both, usually in that order.

**Slide 8 — When the product certification is the right answer**
The advert names the package and your hands are not fast in it. You are being handed a live model next month. Your employer's framework requires a specific certification for a grade. You need a credential from an accredited body today. In all four cases, take that route.

**Slide 9 — What PCL-AI examines**
The PCI AI Project Controls Leader (PCL-AI) examines the discipline the file expresses: measurement, forecasting, control and the reporting that follows, across 13 domains and 61 knowledge areas, on a Body of Knowledge weighted 40 / 40 / 20 across finance and reporting, project management and governed AI.

**Slide 10 — What PCI does not claim**
PCI is a new, independent certifying body. It is not accredited by ANAB, UKAS, IAS or any other ISO/IEC 17024 accreditation body, and does not claim to be. The scheme is built with reference to ISO/IEC 17024 principles. No pass rates, no salary claims, no holder numbers. The Body of Knowledge is published in full — read it before you decide anything.
