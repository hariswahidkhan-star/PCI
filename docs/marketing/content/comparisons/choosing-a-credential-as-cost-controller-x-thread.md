# A £0.45m accrual, a £1.0m forecast, and one choice

*X / Threads thread — 9 posts, each under 280 characters and each able to stand alone. The link goes in the first reply. Character counts are for production; X counts any URL as 23 characters.*

**Post 1/9 — the hook** (168 characters)
An invoice that has not arrived is worth about a million pounds on this job.

Earned value £6.60m. Actual cost as booked £6.30m. Work done and not yet invoiced: £0.45m.

**Post 2/9 — two indices** (172 characters)
As booked: 6.60 ÷ 6.30 = 1.048. The job is outperforming.

With the accrual: 6.60 ÷ 6.75 = 0.978. The job is behind.

Same month, same site, same work. One missing invoice.

**Post 3/9 — the forecast** (186 characters)
Budget at completion £15.0m.

At 1.048: outturn 15.0 ÷ 1.048 = £14.32m.
At 0.978: outturn 15.0 ÷ 0.978 = £15.34m.

Just over £1.0m apart, from a cut-off gap rather than anything on site.

**Post 4/9 — where it goes next** (204 characters)
It does not stop at the cost report.

That outturn is the denominator when progress is measured by costs incurred against total expected costs. Move it and you move recognised revenue and reported margin.

**Post 5/9 — the credential question** (169 characters)
So: does the examination you are weighing cover that step, or hand over at the cost report?

Both are legitimate designs. You just need to know which one you are buying.

**Post 6/9 — what each category examines** (233 characters)
Cost and scheduling credentials examine estimating, cost control, planning and schedule analysis.

Accountancy and finance qualifications examine recognition, measurement, disclosure and financial control.

Each publishes a boundary.

**Post 7/9 — the accrual between them** (184 characters)
The accrual sits between those two subjects and is examined by neither as a matter of course.

That is a fact about how professions were organised, not a failing in anybody's syllabus.

**Post 8/9 — when the shorter route wins** (211 characters)
If you own the cost report and a commercial manager takes it from there, a credential examining estimating and cost control examines your job. Take it.

If a grading matrix names one, take the named one instead.
