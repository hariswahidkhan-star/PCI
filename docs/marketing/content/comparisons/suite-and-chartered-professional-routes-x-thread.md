# One month, one job, three right answers to the same question

*X / Threads thread — 7 posts, each under 280 characters and each able to stand alone. The link sits in the first reply, not in the thread. X counts any URL as 23 characters.*

**Post 1/7 — the hook** (182 characters)
Three people were asked how much work had been done last month.
£13.92m. £12.60m. £11.97m.
Same job, same month, three correct answers, and £1.95m between the highest and the lowest.

**Post 2/7 — the recognised figure** (211 characters)
Fixed price £24.0m. Total expected cost £20.0m, so margin £4.0m.
Costs incurred to date £11.6m.
11.6 ÷ 20.0 = 58% on a cost-to-cost measure.
Revenue = 0.58 × 24.0 = £13.92m. Margin to date = 0.58 × 4.0 = £2.32m.

**Post 3/7 — the certified figure and the cash** (230 characters)
The certified valuation for the same month is £12.60m.
Retention at 5% withholds £0.63m, so £11.97m is payable.
The £1.32m between revenue and certification is a contract asset: measured, not yet an unconditional right to payment.

**Post 4/7 — three rulebooks** (217 characters)
Measurement produces the valuation.
Progress towards satisfying the obligation produces the revenue.
The payment terms produce the cash.
Nobody is wrong. Somebody still has to explain the gaps to a director this week.

**Post 5/7 — what a chartered route assesses** (258 characters)
Practised competence, evidenced over years, judged by peers against a competence framework, with a code of conduct attached.
If a client, a contract or a grade asks for chartered standing, take that route. Nothing examined substitutes for it, including ours.

**Post 6/7 — what an examination assesses** (204 characters)
A different question: can you do the work on a published syllabus, on the day, marked against published objectives.
It states a tested scope. It cannot tell you somebody ran a hard job well for six years.

**Post 7/7 — where the PCI suite sits** (271 characters)
PCL-AI, PFL-AI and PML-AI examine 13/16/16 domains and 61/61/63 knowledge areas, weighted 40/40/20 across finance and reporting, delivery and governed AI — the join the three numbers sit in.
PCI is new and not accredited — read the syllabus and judge it.
