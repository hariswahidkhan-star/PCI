# Audit credentials and PFL-AI: where each of them sits

*X / Threads thread — 7 posts, each under 280 characters and each able to stand alone. The link sits in the final post. Character counts are for production; X counts any URL as 23 characters, so the live figure runs lower.*

**Post 1/7 — the hook** (237 characters)
The accrual was raised with evidence. The invoice arrived the next month. Nobody reversed the accrual.

Same cost counted twice. £0.45m of revenue landed in the wrong period, and every control in the process operated exactly as designed.

**Post 2/7 — the numbers** (234 characters)
Contract price £19.0m. Total expected cost £16.0m. Progress measured cost-to-cost.

Accrual raised in month 6: £380k, supported by delivery records. In month 7 the invoice posts at £380k and the accrual stays.

Cost overstated: £380k.

**Post 3/7 — the arithmetic** (211 characters)
£380k on £16.0m of expected cost = 2.375 percentage points of measured progress.

2.375% of a £19.0m price = £0.45m of revenue recognised a month early.

No cash moved. No approval was missing. One reversal was.

**Post 4/7 — what audit credentials examine** (271 characters)
Audit credentials examine governance, risk, control and the assurance process: how evidence is obtained, how independence holds, how a conclusion is reached.

That is examined thoroughly and by design. It is a different question from whether this month's figure is right.

**Post 5/7 — the other question** (248 characters)
Was the accrual reversed against the invoice it anticipated? Was the survey date the ledger date? Is the rule of credit still the rule that was agreed?

Those live in the cost ledger and the control account, and somebody has to be examined on them.

**Post 6/7 — where PFL-AI sits** (254 characters)
PCI AI Project Finance Leader (PFL-AI) is examined on that chain: evidence at source, the accrual, the cut-off, the forecast and what it does to revenue.

Disclosure: credentialfinder.org is operated by Project Controls Institute Global, which awards it.

**Post 7/7 — the honest part** (276 characters)
If your job is assurance and independence, take the audit route. PFL-AI confers no opinion, no independence and no assurance standing, and does not try to.

PCI is new and not accredited — read the syllabus and judge it.
