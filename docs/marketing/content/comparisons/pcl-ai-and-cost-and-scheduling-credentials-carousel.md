# Cost and scheduling credentials: where PCL-AI sits

*LinkedIn document carousel. 11 slides at 1080 × 1350. One idea per slide. Slide 3 carries the
arithmetic, because slide 3 is the one people screenshot.*

---

### Slide 1 — cover
**£190k of overrun that was five days of calendar.**
Not five days of work.
*Alt text: title slide reading "£190k of overrun that was five days of calendar, not five days
of work".*

### Slide 2 — the set-up
1,000 metres of pipe. £1,000 a metre. Budget at completion £1.0m.

The planner cuts the schedule on the 25th.
Finance cuts the ledger on the 30th.

Both did their own job correctly.
*Alt text: slide describing a pipeline package with a schedule cut on the 25th and a ledger cut
on the 30th.*

### Slide 3 — the arithmetic
**800 m in the ground on the 25th → EV £800k**
**£950k booked to the 30th → AC £950k**

CPI = 800 ÷ 950 = **0.84**
EAC = 1,000 ÷ 0.84 = **£1.19m**

Forecast overrun: **£190k**
*Alt text: calculation slide showing CPI of 0.84 and an estimate at completion of £1.19m against
a £1.0m budget.*

### Slide 4 — the correction
Recut both sides to the 30th.

950 m in the ground. £950k earned. £950k spent.

CPI **1.00**. EAC **£1.0m**.

Nothing on site changed. A date changed.
*Alt text: slide showing the same package recut to a common date, giving CPI 1.00 and an EAC
equal to budget.*

### Slide 5 — the second version of the same problem
40 valves at £10,000. Twenty delivered. None installed.

0/100 earning rule → EV **£0**
50/50 earning rule → EV **£100k**

Same site. Same steel. Same day.
*Alt text: slide comparing two earning rules producing £0 and £100k of earned value for the same
delivered valves.*

### Slide 6 — what the category examines
Cost and scheduling credentials examine estimating, cost control, planning, schedule analysis
and the economics of the asset life cycle.

They examine that subject thoroughly, and are built to.
*Alt text: slide listing what cost and scheduling credentials set out to examine.*

### Slide 7 — where the boundary sits
The point where a delivery number becomes a reported number sits outside their scope — as it
does for every credential in that category.

A scope boundary is a design decision, not a failing.
*Alt text: slide stating that the reporting handover sits outside the scope of the cost and
scheduling category by design.*

### Slide 8 — what PCL-AI examines
**13 domains. 61 knowledge areas.**

The control cycle, how its numbers become reported numbers, and the governed use of AI inside
that cycle.
*Alt text: slide summarising PCL-AI as 13 domains and 61 knowledge areas covering both sides of
the finance and delivery line.*

### Slide 9 — when this is not for you
Estimating is your whole job → take the depth of the cost and scheduling route.

You need an accredited credential today → go where accreditation already exists.

Your employer mandates one → take that one.
*Alt text: slide listing three situations in which a different credential is the right answer.*

PCI is a new, independent certifying body. Not accredited by ANAB, UKAS, IAS or any other

Built with reference to ISO/IEC 17024 principles.
