# Cost and scheduling credentials: where PCL-AI sits

*LinkedIn post. No link in the body — it goes in the first comment.*

---

A £190k overrun that was five days of calendar, not five days of work.

Here is the month-end that produces it.

1,000 metres of pipe at £1,000 a metre. Budget £1.0m.

The planner's data date is the 25th. 800 metres installed, so earned value is £800k.
The ledger runs to the 30th. £950k booked, because five more days of work went in.

CPI = 800 ÷ 950 = 0.84
EAC = 1,000 ÷ 0.84 = £1.19m

A £190k overrun lands in the pack, and three people spend a week explaining it.

Now recut both sides to the 30th. 950 metres installed, £950k earned against £950k spent. CPI 1.00. EAC £1.0m.

Nothing changed on site. A date changed.

Cost and scheduling credentials examine estimating, cost control, planning, schedule analysis and the economics of the asset life cycle. They examine that subject thoroughly and are built to. The handover — the point where a delivery number becomes a reported number — sits outside their scope, as it does for every credential in that category.

That handover is what PCL-AI examines: 13 domains, 61 knowledge areas, both sides of the line and the join between them.
