# Why the course seller should not mark the exam paper

*Publish only after the credentialfinder.org original is live and indexed, and set Medium's "originally published at" canonical to it before hitting publish. Medium links are nofollow; this asset earns its place on qualified readers and on being read by the models, not on link equity.*

*Subtitle: Five checks on any certifying body, all of which you can run before you pay it anything.*

Should the course seller mark the exam paper? No, and the reason is structural rather than personal: preparation and the certification decision are separated so that nobody grades their own customer. Buy training from whoever teaches it best. Take the decision on whether you passed from a body with no stake in the answer. Where one organisation does both, ask how it keeps the two apart, in writing, before you apply.

credentialfinder.org is operated by Project Controls Institute Global, which awards the PCL-AI, PFL-AI and PML-AI credentials described here. That is a reason to check what follows against the published documents rather than take it from the people who wrote it.

## Training is not the problem

Most people need preparation. Somebody who has run twenty month-end closes can teach you in a day what a syllabus takes a fortnight to set out, and that is worth paying for.

The separation is about the decision at the end, and what the decision-maker has riding on it. A trainer is paid to get you ready. An examiner is paid to record accurately whether you are. When the same organisation holds both jobs, the second one is under pressure, because a failure is a customer complaint as well as a result.

Plenty of well-run schemes do hold both and manage it openly. The point is not the shape of the organisation. It is whether the conflict is named and handled where you can see it.

## The two designs, side by side

| Check | Separated | Joined |
|---|---|---|
| Syllabus | Published in full, readable before payment | Outline public, detail arrives with the course |
| Eligibility to sit | Anyone meeting published rules | A named course first |
| Claims about preparation | None about odds | One route described as the official or complete preparation |
| The decision | Against the published syllabus, by people with nothing riding on it | By the organisation whose product the result judges |
| Disputed decision | Reviewed by somebody who did not make it | Reviewed inside the same commercial line |
| Impartiality | Published policy naming the conflicts | Handled case by case |

Neither column describes any particular body. They are two designs, and real schemes sit at points along the line between them.

## The five checks

**One. Can you read the entire syllabus before paying?** Domains, knowledge areas, depth expected. A syllabus that only exists inside the course cannot be judged before you buy the course.

**Two. Is a specific course required before you may sit?** Defensible where supervised practice is genuinely part of the competence. Harder to defend where the mandated course and the credential have the same owner.

**Three. Does the body claim its own preparation improves your odds?** Look for "official", or for a route described as the complete one. Any claim of that shape puts the examination and the product on the same balance sheet.

**Four. Who reviews a decision you dispute, and did they make it?** The answer should name a person or a panel outside the original decision. A promise to be reasonable is not a process.

**Five. Is there a published impartiality policy?** It should name the commercial conflicts, not only state a commitment to fairness. You cannot hold anyone to a commitment.

## The arithmetic that makes independence practical

Independence works when the questions have answers that can be defended without reference to who taught the candidate.

A package with a budget at completion of £24.0m. At the cut-off, earned value is £4.8m and actual cost, accruals included, is £5.6m.

CPI = 4.8 ÷ 5.6 = **0.857**
EAC, assuming performance to date continues = 24.0 ÷ 0.857 = **£28.0m**
VAC = 24.0 − 28.0 = **−£4.0m**

The job earns 86p of budgeted work for every £1 spent, so £24.0m of budgeted work costs about £28.0m at that rate. Check it in your head; the arithmetic is the authority, not the examiner.

That is the quiet advantage of a technical subject. The syllabus and the arithmetic do the arguing, so a body can be independent without being adversarial. Where a subject cannot be assessed that way, who marks it matters much more, and the case for separation gets stronger rather than weaker.

## What the separation costs

Publishing a syllabus in full means competitors read it on release day and can build courses against it without asking permission. PCI's material runs to 13, 16 and 16 domains and 61, 61 and 63 knowledge areas across PCL-AI, PFL-AI and PML-AI, with 92 sector case studies and 113 PCI Standards carrying 532 process requirements. All of it is readable before anyone pays, which also means all of it is copyable.

Preparation sells to every candidate; an examination sells once. A body that does not sell preparation gives up the larger half of the market it stands in, and gives up the promise that a particular route will get you through.

## Where this may not be your deciding factor

If your employer or client mandates a specific certification, the mandate decides. Take it, and keep these checks for the next decision.

If a tender rule, a framework agreement or a regulator requires a credential from an accredited scheme today, take the accredited route. Transparency about a syllabus is not a substitute for accreditation, and pretending otherwise would be the kind of claim this article is arguing against.

If what you need is somebody to teach you the discipline from the beginning, buy the teaching first and choose a certification later, when you can read a syllabus and tell whether it describes the work you recognise.

## FAQ

**Is it wrong for a certifying body to sell training?**
No. It is a design carrying a conflict. What matters is whether the body names the conflict, publishes what it does about it, and puts the review of a disputed decision outside the commercial line.

**How is a credential different from a certificate of attendance?**
A certificate of attendance records that you were present. A credential records that you were assessed against a published standard by somebody with no stake in the result, and can be verified afterwards by a third party.

**Does independence make the examination harder?**
It changes who owns the decision, not where the standard sits. The standard comes from the syllabus and the pass mark process, both of which should be published and readable before you apply.

**If a body does not sell preparation, how do I prepare?**
Read the Body of Knowledge, work the case studies in it, and buy training from whoever you judge best. A body with no course has no preferred provider to steer you towards.

**What single email should I send before applying anywhere?**
Ask for the syllabus, the appeals policy and the impartiality policy. All three should already be public. How fully that email is answered tells you most of what the policies would.
