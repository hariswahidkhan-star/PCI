# The margin you did not build: 7 posts on a reforecast

*X / Threads thread — 7 posts, each under 280 characters and each able to stand alone. No link in the thread itself; the link goes in the first reply. X counts any URL as 23 characters.*

**Post 1/7 — the hook** (159 characters)
£0.63m came off the reported margin this month. Nobody made an accounting error.

An estimate you did not build moved, and the whole of that swing followed it.

**Post 2/7 — the figures** (152 characters)
Contract price £33.0m. Cost incurred to date £12.0m. Estimate of total cost at the last review £30.0m.

Progress measured by cost against expected cost.

**Post 3/7 — before** (138 characters)
Progress = 12.0 ÷ 30.0 = 40.0%.
Revenue = 0.40 × 33.0 = £13.20m.
Margin = 13.20 − 12.00 = £1.20m.

An ordinary month, reported and closed.

**Post 4/7 — after** (174 characters)
Delivery revises expected total cost to £31.5m: a rate above allowance, a subcontractor replaced, three weeks of rework.

12.0 ÷ 31.5 = 38.1%. Revenue £12.57m. Margin £0.57m.

**Post 5/7 — and the balance sheet** (162 characters)
Invoiced £11.0m, so the contract asset is 12.57 − 11.00 = £1.57m.

It then ages, and its ageing is a conversation about entitlement rather than one with a ledger.

**Post 6/7 — whose subject is this** (230 characters)
Accountancy and finance qualifications examine recognition, measurement, disclosure and control, properly.

Cost and scheduling credentials examine how that estimate is built and maintained. A project accountant sits between them.
