# Choosing a credential as a scheduler moving into controls

Choosing a credential as a scheduler moving into controls means covering the three things the move adds to your signature: an earning rule, a forecast and a cut-off date. Scheduling credentials examine schedule development, maintenance and analysis thoroughly, and they are built to. The three new items sit beyond that boundary, and the credential worth your evenings is the one that examines them.

> Global, which awards the PCL-AI, PFL-AI and PML-AI credentials described here. Read the

## What changes when you cross into controls

| What you sign now | What you signed as a scheduler | Which category examines the new part |
|---|---|---|
| The earning rule for each work type | Progress against the programme | The established project controls certifications |
| Earned value for the period | Percentage complete on activities | The established project controls certifications |
| The estimate at completion | Forecast dates | Cost and scheduling credentials |
| The cut-off calendar | The data date | Examined systematically almost nowhere |
| Variance explanation to a sponsor | Programme narrative | The established project controls certifications |
| The integrated baseline | The schedule baseline | Cost and scheduling credentials |

The left column is a bigger job than the middle one, and the difference is not effort. It is consequence: each of those items produces a number somebody outside delivery relies on.

## The earning rule is where a scheduler's month goes wrong

Two rules, one month, same work in the ground.

A package of activities worth **£1.2m** starts in the period and finishes next period. Under a 50/50 rule, half its value is earned on start. Under a 0/100 rule, nothing is earned until completion.

Total earned value for the period under 50/50 = **£3.0m**.
Under 0/100 = **£2.4m**.
Actual cost in the period = **£2.8m**.

CPI under 50/50 = 3.0 ÷ 2.8 = **1.071**. The job is outperforming.
CPI under 0/100 = 2.4 ÷ 2.8 = **0.857**. The job is behind.

Nothing physical differs between those two months. A rule chosen at baseline, often by whoever set the template up, decides which story the report tells. Choosing that rule, applying it consistently, and being able to explain why a run of 50/50 packages flatters an early month is a controls competence rather than a scheduling one — and it sits under everything in [what earned value management actually measures](https://projectcontrolsinstitute.org/earned-value-management).

Now carry it one step further. The index feeds a forecast, the forecast is the denominator in a percentage complete, and the percentage complete becomes recognised revenue. A template decision has reached a set of accounts.

Nobody on that chain was examined on the whole of it. A planning engineer is examined on float and progress measurement. A chartered accountant is examined on when revenue may be recognised and what a provision must satisfy. Neither examination covers the handover in the middle, and that handover is the move you are making.

## What each category examines, in its own terms

Cost and scheduling credentials examine estimating, cost control, planning and schedule analysis. If you are staying on the programme side, that is your subject and it is examined in depth.

The established project controls certifications examine measurement, progress, forecasting and the control of delivery. That is where the three new signature items live.

Product certifications for scheduling software examine the operation of a specific tool. That is a genuine and marketable skill, and it is a different claim from competence in the discipline the tool serves. Both are worth having and they should not be confused when you are building a CV.

## When staying on the scheduling side is the right answer

If you like the programme work and the move is somebody else's idea, say so and take the scheduling route. Deep schedule analysis is a scarce skill, it is examined thoroughly by its own category, and specialising in it is a career rather than a waypoint.

If a client's prequalification or an internal grading matrix names a certification, take the named one first. Fit does not beat a stated requirement.

And if you have not yet run a full period end in a controls seat, wait. The examinations that cover this ground ask what you would do when the index and the site disagree, and that answer comes from having been in the room when they did. What the wider role covers day to day is set out in [what a project controls engineer actually does](https://pciworld.org/what-does-a-project-controls-engineer-do).

## How to decide

Take your current programme and answer four questions in writing. Which earning rule applies to each work type, and who chose it. What the cut-off dates are for progress and for cost, and whether they are the same date. What forecast your progress feeds, and who signs it. And which of those you could defend to a finance director next Thursday.

The questions you cannot answer are the syllabus you need. If all four come easily, you have already made the move and the credential is evidence rather than education, which is still a fair reason to sit one.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is a scheduling credential wasted if I move into controls?**
No. Schedule analysis stays in the job and stays scarce, and a controls manager who can actually interrogate a programme is worth more than one who takes the dates on trust. It becomes a component of a wider role rather than the whole of it.

**How long does the move take?**
Usually a year of period ends, because the learning is cyclical rather than linear. You meet the same four artefacts every month and understand them a little better each time. Preparing for an examination during that year works well; sitting one before it starts usually does not.

**Do I need to learn accounting?**
You need the handshake, not the profession: what a percentage complete becomes, what an accrual is for, why a cut-off gap moves an index. That is a small body of material with a high return, and it is set out in [the finance and delivery overlap](https://credentialfinder.org/the-finance-and-delivery-overlap).

**Does a tool certificate help with the move?**
It helps you do the work and it does not evidence the judgement the new role needs. Product certifications examine operating a specific piece of software, which is a real skill sold in similar language to certification of the discipline. Know which one a line on your CV is claiming.

**Which credential do I choose if I want both sides open?**
Pick on the decisions you expect to sign in two years rather than the ones you sign now, and use a grid rather than a ranking. The method is in [choosing between two credentials without a league table](https://credentialfinder.org/choosing-between-two-credentials).
