# PM certifications and PML-AI: where each of them sits

*X / Threads thread — 7 posts, each under 280 characters and each able to stand alone. The link sits in the final post. Character counts are for production; X counts any URL as 23 characters, so the live figure runs lower.*

**Post 1/7 — the hook** (207 characters)
A project manager spent £600k to pull six weeks out of a programme. Good decision, defensible on its own terms.

It also moved this month's reported result by £0.30m, and he found that out from someone else.

**Post 2/7 — the setting** (186 characters)
Fixed price £26.0m. Expected cost at completion £23.4m, so expected margin £2.6m. Costs incurred £12.87m, which is 55% on a cost-to-cost measure.

Time-related cost runs at £180k a week.

**Post 3/7 — the decision** (195 characters)
Accelerate: £600k of extra resource, six weeks saved.

Time-related cost avoided = 6 × 180k = £1.08m.
New expected cost = 23.4 + 0.6 − 1.08 = £22.92m.
New expected margin = 26.0 − 22.92 = £3.08m.

**Post 4/7 — the bit nobody briefed him on** (210 characters)
Lower expected cost raises measured progress: 12.87 ÷ 22.92 = 56.15%.

Cumulative margin = 0.5615 × 3.08 = £1.73m, against £1.43m before (0.55 × 2.6).

£0.30m into this month's result, from a schedule decision.

**Post 5/7 — what PM certifications examine** (265 characters)
Project management certifications examine managing projects across people, process and the business environment. Scope, schedule, cost, quality, risk, procurement, leadership.

Examined thoroughly, and built to be. The accounting consequence is a different subject.

**Post 6/7 — where PML-AI sits** (267 characters)
PCI Project Management Leader – AI (PML-AI) is examined on a Body of Knowledge weighted 40 / 40 / 20 across finance and reporting, project management and governed AI.

Disclosure: credentialfinder.org is operated by Project Controls Institute Global, which awards it.

**Post 7/7 — the honest part** (270 characters)
If your employer names a certification, or you need an accredited credential today, take that route. It is the right answer and not a close call.

PCI is new and not accredited — read the syllabus and judge it.
