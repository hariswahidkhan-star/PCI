# What makes a credential examinable, and how to check

A syllabus is examinable when every line names something a candidate must be able to **do**, with an object specific enough that the marker knows what data the candidate was handed, at a stated depth. The verbs decide it. "Understands estimate at completion" cannot be marked consistently. "Computes an estimate at completion from a stated budget, earned value and actual cost, and states the assumption the method carries" can.

A disclosure before anything else, because it matters to how you read this: credentialfinder.org is operated by Project Controls Institute Global, which awards the PCL-AI, PFL-AI and PML-AI credentials referred to below. So run the four tests on us as well as on everybody else. They work either way, which is the point of writing them down.

## The four tests

**One. An observable verb.** Calculate, reconcile, classify, select and justify, reject and say why. Not understand, appreciate, be aware of. You cannot photograph understanding.

**Two. A bounded object.** The line tells you what arrives with the question. "Progress measurement" is a subject heading. "Selects an earning rule for a partially complete work package and defends the choice against the data available" is a task.

**Three. A stated depth.** Recall, application and analysis of one topic are three different examinations. A syllabus that does not say which one it means has not decided.

**Four. A defensible key.** Where judgement is required, the scheme states what makes an answer right, and lets a candidate read that before paying.

## One line, rewritten

Unexaminable: *Understands estimate at completion.*

Examinable: *Given a budget at completion of £48.0m, earned value of £14.4m and actual cost of £16.0m, computes the estimate at completion on the assumption that current cost performance continues, and states the result.*

Cost performance index = 14.4 ÷ 16.0 = 0.90. Estimate at completion = 48.0 ÷ 0.90 = **£53.33m**. Change one word — remaining work delivered at the budgeted rate — and it becomes 16.0 + (48.0 − 14.4) = **£49.60m**. Same three figures, £3.73m apart. The candidate has to know which question was asked. That is markable. "Understands" is not.

## What examinability does not tell you

Not difficulty, not standing, not whether a hiring manager will accept it. Cost and scheduling credentials, accountancy and finance qualifications, audit credentials and project management certifications all publish examinable outcomes in their own subjects, and they examine those subjects properly. This test tells you what a credential sets out to make you able to do. It ranks nobody.

## Where this is not your deciding question

If your employer mandates a named certification, or a tender scores an accredited one, examinability is interesting and the mandate is binding. Take the one you are required to hold, and read syllabuses later.

PCI is a new, independent certifying body. It is not accredited by ANAB, UKAS, IAS or any other ISO/IEC 17024 accreditation body, and does not claim to be. The scheme is built with reference to ISO/IEC 17024 principles. Read the published Body of Knowledge before you decide anything.
