# Scope or prestige, decided with ten job advertisements

*X / Threads thread — 9 posts, each under 280 characters and each able to stand alone. The link goes in the first reply. Character counts are for production; X counts any URL as 23 characters.*

**Post 1/9 — the hook** (145 characters)
Recognition gets you the interview. Scope decides the year that follows. Two axes, and most people only ever run the one that is easier to check.

**Post 2/9 — measuring recognition, part one** (206 characters)
Recognition is countable in an hour. Open ten current adverts for the exact role, in the exact market you want it in. List every credential each one names. Note whether it sits under essential or desirable.

**Post 3/9 — measuring recognition, part two** (202 characters)
Then do the same with ten profiles of people already in that job. Two counts, one hour. The result is about your market rather than about anybody's marketing, including a certifying body's about itself.

**Post 4/9 — the scope audit** (225 characters)
Scope takes longer, so it gets skipped. List what you personally sign: forecast, valuation, percentage complete, accrual, change position. Open the syllabus. Mark each one examined, assumed or excluded. Count rows, not pages.

**Post 5/9 — the arithmetic, part one** (235 characters)
Why scope is not a soft axis. Progress measured to the 25th, costs booked to the 30th. EV £4.20m. AC £4.65m, of which £0.35m is spend on work earned next month.

As reported: CPI = 4.20 ÷ 4.65 = 0.903
Aligned: CPI = 4.20 ÷ 4.30 = 0.977

**Post 6/9 — the arithmetic, part two** (187 characters)
On a £12.0m budget at completion: 12.0 ÷ 0.903 = £13.29m. 12.0 ÷ 0.977 = £12.28m. Just over a million pounds, produced by a calendar rather than by performance. Nobody did anything wrong.

**Post 7/9 — when recognition wins** (220 characters)
When recognition should win, plainly. A prequalification list naming a certification. A grading matrix naming one. A new country where your CV gets thirty seconds. Scope arguments you never get to make are worth nothing.

**Post 8/9 — when scope wins** (217 characters)
When scope should win. You already get the interviews and the problem is the work: a forecast you cannot defend, a month-end that moves for reasons nobody can explain. Recognition adds nothing you do not already have.

**Post 9/9 — ours, plainly** (264 characters)
Ours: each PCI Body of Knowledge is 40/40/20 finance and reporting, project management, governed AI. That describes the syllabus, not an exam weighting. credentialfinder.org is operated by PCI Global. PCI is new and not accredited — read the syllabus and judge it.
