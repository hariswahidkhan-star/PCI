# Same month, two rules, £5.04m apart: why currency counts

*X / Threads thread — 7 posts, each under 280 characters and each able to stand alone. No link in the thread; the link sits in the first reply. X counts a URL as 23 characters.*

**Post 1/7 — the hook** (202 characters)
A certificate records a decision made on a date.

Recertification is what keeps that date meaning something. It says the holder is current, not that they once were.

Everything else is a renewal notice.

**Post 2/7 — why currency is a real question** (232 characters)
Fixed price £42.0m. Costs to date £22.8m. Expected total cost £38.0m.

Cost-to-cost progress = 22.8 ÷ 38.0 = 60%
Revenue = 0.60 × 42.0 = £25.2m

By units completed the job is 48% done: 0.48 × 42.0 = £20.16m

Same data. £5.04m apart.

**Post 3/7 — what moves under you** (230 characters)
Which measure faithfully depicts transfer of control, what evidence supports it, and what the tooling does with it are all answers that move over a career.

A certificate issued years ago is silent on whether the holder moved too.

**Post 4/7 — the two mechanisms** (249 characters)
Recorded CPD measures continued contact with the subject. Re-examination measures current command of the syllabus.

CPD fails when hours are counted and relevance is not. Re-examination fails when the paper never changes and it tests recall instead.

**Post 5/7 — what good CPD looks like** (244 characters)
Mapped, not counted: which knowledge area did this touch?
Evidenced, not asserted: keep the procedure you rewrote.
Sampled, not audited to death.

And include the parts of the subject you avoid. More of what you already do well changes nothing.

**Post 6/7 — the check employers actually use** (250 characters)
Does public verification show status on the date it is checked — current, expired, suspended, withdrawn — or only that somebody once passed?

A register of everyone who ever qualified is a list, not a verification. Check it before you sit, not after.

**Post 7/7 — the disclosure** (274 characters)
If your employer's framework recognises a particular scheme's cycle, that cycle is the one that matters to you.
