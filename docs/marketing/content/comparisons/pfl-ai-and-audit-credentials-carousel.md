# Audit credentials and PFL-AI: what each one examines

*LinkedIn document post — 10 slides, 1080 × 1350. No link in the body; the link goes in the first comment.*

**Post caption (the first two lines carry the post):**

Two teams, the same 1,200 drawings, the same evidence, the same signatures. Their earned value figures were £0.19m apart.

Both controls operated exactly as designed. Ten slides on the difference between assurance and measurement, and which credential examines which.

---

**Slide 1 — Two honest questions**
Assurance asks whether a control existed, operated as designed and left evidence. Project finance asks whether the measure that control operated on is the right measure. Both are legitimate. They are not the same question.

**Slide 2 — The setting**
An engineering control account. Budget £3.0m. 1,200 deliverables. Earned value is claimed on a rule of credit, agreed at baseline, applied every month, reviewed and signed.

**Slide 3 — The arithmetic**
Deliverables **issued for review: 720** (60.0%). Deliverables **approved: 340** (28.33%).

**Rule A — 40% at issue, 60% at approval:**
(0.40 × 0.600) + (0.60 × 0.2833) = 0.240 + 0.170 = **0.410**
Earned value = 0.410 × £3.0m = **£1.23m**

**Rule B — 20% at issue, 80% at approval:**
(0.20 × 0.600) + (0.80 × 0.2833) = 0.120 + 0.227 = **0.347**
Earned value = 0.347 × £3.0m = **£1.04m**

**£0.19m of difference, and both rules were applied perfectly.**

**Slide 4 — What the difference actually is**
It is not an error and it is not a control failure. It is a judgement about how much value transfers when a drawing is issued rather than approved. Somebody made that judgement at baseline, often quickly, often years ago.

**Slide 5 — What audit credentials examine**
Governance, risk and control, and the assurance process: planning an engagement, obtaining and evaluating evidence, preserving independence, reaching and reporting a conclusion. Deep subject, examined thoroughly, built to be.

**Slide 6 — What sits outside that scope, by design**
Which rule of credit reflects value transferred. Whether the survey date matches the ledger date. What a forecast movement does to recognised revenue. These are management judgements made on the project side.

**Slide 7 — Where PFL-AI sits**
PCI AI Project Finance Leader (PFL-AI) is examined on that side: measurement rules, accrual evidence, cut-off, the estimate at completion and the accounting consequences that follow it. 16 domains, 61 knowledge areas.

**Slide 8 — Disclosure, stated here rather than in small print**
credentialfinder.org, where the full comparison sits, is operated by Project Controls Institute Global, which awards PFL-AI. The Body of Knowledge is published in full before anyone pays anything, which is the fair way to check a claim like the one on slide 7.

**Slide 9 — When the audit route is the right one**
If independence is the point of your job, if you work in an assurance function, if you audit systems and processes rather than build the numbers inside them, take an audit credential. PFL-AI confers no opinion and no assurance standing, and does not try to.

**Slide 10 — What we cannot claim**
PCI is a new, independent certifying body. It is not accredited by ANAB, UKAS, IAS or any other ISO/IEC 17024 accreditation body, and does not claim to be. The scheme is built with reference to ISO/IEC 17024 principles. Read the published Body of Knowledge before you decide anything.
