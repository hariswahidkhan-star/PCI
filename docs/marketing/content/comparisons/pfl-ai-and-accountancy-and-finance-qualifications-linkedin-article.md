# Where PFL-AI sits against accountancy qualifications

Accountancy and finance qualifications examine recognition, measurement, disclosure, financial
analysis and control. If you want to be a qualified accountant, that is the route and there is
no substitute for it. PCI AI Project Finance Leader (PFL-AI) examines something narrower and
different: the point where project delivery data becomes the financial information a business
reports.

Disclosure before the argument. credentialfinder.org is operated by Project Controls Institute
Global, which awards the PCL-AI, PFL-AI and PML-AI credentials described here. I am writing
about my own employer's scheme, so weigh it accordingly and go and read the syllabus.

## The £100k that turned on a delivery note

A contract worth £10m, with £8m of budgeted cost and £2m of expected margin. Progress is measured
by cost incurred against total expected cost, which is a common and perfectly proper method on
this kind of work.

At the month-end, cost to date is £4.0m.

- Progress = 4.0 ÷ 8.0 = **50%**
- Revenue recognised = 50% × 10.0 = **£5.0m**
- Margin recognised = 5.0 − 4.0 = **£1.0m**

Now £400k of switchgear is delivered to site and posted to cost. It has not been installed.

Include it in the progress calculation and:

- Progress = 4.4 ÷ 8.0 = **55%**
- Revenue = 55% × 10.0 = **£5.5m**
- Margin = 5.5 − 4.4 = **£1.1m**

Treat those materials as not depicting progress — because sitting in a compound is not
construction — and the calculation runs:

- Revenue = 5.0 + 0.4 = **£5.4m**
- Margin = 5.4 − 4.4 = **£1.0m**

Same site, same steel, same day. **£100k of reported margin** decided by whether anyone
established what "delivered" meant, and told the person doing the calculation.

## Which side of that is examined where

The treatment question — whether a cost that does not depict progress may drive recognition — is
squarely an accountancy question. Accountancy and finance qualifications examine recognition,
measurement, disclosure, financial analysis and control, and they examine that question
thoroughly.

The input to it is not an accountancy question. Whether the switchgear is installed, whether the
progress claim matches the physical work, whether the cost-to-date figure includes commitments
that have not been earned — that comes out of the control cycle, and it arrives at the finance
team as a number in a spreadsheet with nobody's judgement attached.

Neither category sets out to examine the join, and the join is where the money goes. That is
not a criticism of either. A scope boundary is a design decision: these qualifications examine
their own subject far better than a hybrid ever could, and they are built to.

## What PFL-AI examines

PFL-AI covers 16 domains and 61 knowledge areas. It examines the reporting side of a project
from the delivery side inwards: how measured progress becomes recognised revenue, how
commitments and accruals behave at a cut-off, how a forecast at completion becomes a provision,
what evidence supports each of those steps and what has to be recorded when one of them changes.

Two figures worth stating precisely. The Body of Knowledge's proportions are 40 finance and
reporting, 40 project management and 20 governed AI — the Body of Knowledge's proportions, not
an examination weighting. And the calculation content behind PFL-AI and PML-AI is verified by
15,613 machine calculation checks, all passing; PCL-AI has no equivalent suite, so the figure
covers those two credentials only.

Across the three volumes there are 92 sector case studies, and 113 PCI Standards carrying 532
process requirements.

## What PFL-AI is not, said plainly

It is not an accountancy qualification. It is not a route into practice. It confers no right to
sign, certify or audit anything, and nothing PCI publishes is legal, tax or accounting advice.

If your ambition is to qualify as an accountant, work in practice, or take responsibility for a
set of statutory accounts, take an accountancy qualification. That is the correct answer and
nobody at PCI will argue with it.

If your ambition is to audit systems or controls as a specialism, an audit credential examines
governance, risk, control and the assurance process, and that is the category for you.

If you need an accredited credential today — for a framework, an approved list, an immigration
route — go to a body that already holds accreditation.

## The accreditation position

PCI is a new, independent certifying body. It is not accredited by ANAB, UKAS, IAS or any other
ISO/IEC 17024 accreditation body, and does not claim to be. The scheme is built with reference
to ISO/IEC 17024 principles. Read the published Body of Knowledge before you decide anything.

We publish no pass rate, no salary uplift and no holder count either, because we have no honest
way to produce them yet. A new body that lists what it cannot claim is giving you the only kind
of evidence it actually has.

## Who PFL-AI is for

The person who sits between the two vocabularies and gets blamed by both.

The project accountant who is handed a progress percentage and cannot interrogate it. The
finance manager on capital projects who needs to know whether a forecast movement is performance
or a cut-off artefact. The controls lead whose numbers keep being restated downstream and who
wants to understand why before the next review, not after it.

If that is your week, the scope is worth reading. If it is not, one of the categories above will
serve you better and this article has done its job by saying so.

## How to test a finance-facing syllabus before you pay

Open the domain list and look for four things.

Does it name the measurement basis and say what happens when the basis and the physical work
disagree? A syllabus that treats progress as an input it never questions is not examining the
join.

Does it deal with cut-off explicitly — commitments, accruals, retentions — rather than assuming
the ledger is complete on the day the pack is drafted?

Does it say what evidence supports a forecast that becomes a provision, and who is allowed to
change that forecast once it has been used?

Does it treat AI as something to be governed inside the process rather than as a topic bolted
on at the end?

Apply those four tests to every syllabus you are weighing, ours included. If PFL-AI fails them
on your reading, do not buy it — and tell us where, because that is a more useful thing to
receive than an enrolment.

## A test worth running

Take last month's revenue calculation on one contract. Find the cost-to-date figure that drove
it. Then ask two questions: does that figure include anything delivered but not installed, and
does the progress percentage match what the site would say if you walked it today?

If the answers differ, work out the margin effect. On a £10m contract with £8m of cost, every
£400k of misplaced cost moves reported margin by around £100k. That is the size of the thing
neither category was built to examine on its own.
