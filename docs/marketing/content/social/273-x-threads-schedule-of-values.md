# A schedule of values is a payment mechanism, not a plan

*X / Threads thread — 7 posts, each under 280 characters and each able to stand alone. The link sits in the final post. Character counts are for production; X counts any URL as 23 characters, so the live figures run lower.*

**Post 1/7 — the hook** (215 characters)
The payment certificate said 22% complete. The installed quantities said 9%.

Both numbers were right. One was measuring money moved and the other was measuring work done, and only one of them belongs in a forecast.

**Post 2/7 — the definition** (247 characters)
A schedule of values is the contract sum divided into priced line items, agreed before work starts, against which each interim payment is valued.

It is a payment mechanism. It is not a plan, and on its own it is not a progress measurement system.

**Post 3/7 — the arithmetic** (264 characters)
Worked case. Contract £4.20m over 14 lines.

Preliminaries line: £630k. Cost of those preliminaries: £310k. So £320k of value sits early.

Month 2 certified £924k = 22.0% of the contract. Installed quantities say 9%.

Gap = 13 points = £546k of cash ahead of work.

**Post 4/7 — what it does to the cost report** (185 characters)
Take earned value straight off that schedule and EV is £924k against an actual cost of £610k.

CPI = 924 ÷ 610 = 1.51. The job looks like it is printing money.

It is printing invoices.

**Post 5/7 — the fix is two registers** (269 characters)
Value payment on the schedule of values. Value progress on a rule of credit: quantities installed, milestones signed, steps completed against a written scale.

One cut-off for both. Where they disagree beyond a threshold, report the gap rather than reconciling it away.

**Post 6/7 — the finance side of the same line** (259 characters)
This is the overlap where money goes missing. An invoiced amount only measures progress when what has been billed matches what has actually been transferred.

A front-loaded line breaks that link, and the surplus sits as a liability until the work catches up.

**Post 7/7 — what to do on Monday** (289 characters)
Price the schedule of values line by line against the estimate before you sign it, cap how much any single line may carry ahead of its cost, and never let it become the earning rule for revenue.
