# PCL-AI certification: what it covers and how to start

PCL-AI certification is the PCI AI Project Controls Leader credential. It examines 13 domains across 61 knowledge areas, covering project accounting, project management and the governed use of AI, and it is assessed by scenario-based multiple-choice examination. Entry needs around three years of professional experience in any field, with no degree requirement.

## What PCL-AI certification covers

The Body of Knowledge is built in three groups, in a deliberate 40 / 40 / 20 proportion of finance and reporting, project management, and governed AI.

| Group | Domains | Share of the Body of Knowledge |
|---|---|---|
| Project accounting and finance | D1 Foundations of accounting for project controls · D2 Financial reporting and the standards · D3 Budgeting and forecasting · D4 Performance management, variance analysis and management reporting | 40% |
| Project management principles | D5 Cost management and cost control · D6 Earned value management and forecasting · D7 Contracts, commercial management, BoQ, invoicing and revenue · D8 Project management lifecycle · D9 Agile, Scrum and adaptive delivery · D10 Project scheduling · D11 Business process cycles (O2C, P2P and the control environment) · D12 Risk management for project controls | 40% |
| AI knowledge and practical approach | D13 AI for project controls and project management: concepts, tools and practice | 20% |

Those proportions describe the Body of Knowledge, not an examination blueprint. Item counts and the pass mark are set by a job-task analysis and a modified-Angoff standard-setting study before the examination goes live, and they are not published yet.

The proportion itself is deliberate rather than conventional, and [why PCI AI exists](https://projectcontrolsinstitute.org/why-pci-ai) sets out what it is a response to: qualifications that examine measurement or reporting, and never the handover between them.

Governed AI appears twice over: as a domain in its own right, and inside the other twelve, because that is where model output actually lands.

## Who PCL-AI suits, and who it does not

It suits people who are asked to defend a number. Cost engineers, cost controllers, planners, schedulers, project controls managers and project accountants sit inside the intended population.

It suits practitioners who already produce the analysis rather than review someone else's. The examination tests what you can do, so reviewing a forecast every month is weaker preparation than building one.

It does not suit someone looking for a first qualification with no working experience behind it. It is also the wrong choice if your accountability is the financial structure of the deal rather than the delivery of the work, in which case the PCI AI Project Finance Leader (PFL-AI) credential is the better fit.

## What the examination is like

| Feature | PCL-AI examination |
|---|---|
| Format | Scenario-based multiple choice, single best answer |
| Cognitive level | Apply, analyse, evaluate — not recall |
| Delivery | Online under remote proctoring, or at a test centre |
| Standard | Criterion-referenced cut score, no curve and no quota |
| AI coverage | A dedicated domain plus embedded coverage in the others |
| Scheduling | The examination fee opens a 12-month window from the payment date |
| Accommodations | Available on request, arranged before you book |

Criterion-referenced matters more than it sounds. Everyone who demonstrates the required standard passes, however many others sat that week, so your result reflects your competence rather than your ranking.

## A worked item, of the kind the exam sets

A £30m project reports the following at the data date: planned value £12.0m, earned value £12.0m, actual cost £13.5m.

Schedule performance index = EV / PV = 12.0 / 12.0 = **1.00**. Cost performance index = EV / AC = 12.0 / 13.5 = **0.889**.

A recall-based answer stops there and reports that the project is on time and over cost. The applied answer does not.

SPI is cost-weighted, so it rises when any work earns value, including work with plenty of float. The critical path can be 15 days late while SPI reads exactly 1.00, because early, low-risk activities over-earned and masked it.

So the competent next move is to read total float on the driving path before saying anything about schedule, and to report the two independently. Earned value tells you about volume of work; float tells you about the finish date. [The earned value formulas in full](https://projectcontrolsinstitute.org/earned-value-management), including where each index stops being reliable, are the underlying material for items of this kind.

The cost side still needs a forecast. If cost performance to date is the best predictor, EAC = BAC / CPI = 30.0 / 0.889 = **£33.75m**, giving a variance at completion of −£3.75m.

And the recovery question: the to-complete performance index needed to land on budget is (BAC − EV) / (BAC − AC) = 18.0 / 16.5 = **1.09**. A team running at 0.889 must run at 1.09 on everything that remains, which is a 23% improvement in efficiency.

## A worked item from the AI domain

A model flags remaining activities at risk of slipping. The project has 600 activities left, of which 60 will genuinely slip. The model flags 100, and 45 of those do slip.

**Precision** is how many flags were right: 45 / 100 = **0.45**. More than half the flags were false alarms.

**Recall** is how many real slips were caught: 45 / 60 = **0.75**. A quarter were missed entirely.

**F1** is the harmonic mean: 2 × (0.45 × 0.75) / (0.45 + 0.75) = 0.675 / 1.20 = **0.563**.

Now the number a vendor would quote. Correct predictions are 45 true flags plus 485 correctly unflagged activities, so accuracy is 530 / 600 = **88%**.

An 88% accurate model whose flags are wrong 55% of the time will exhaust a planning team in three weeks. Being able to say that, in the meeting, is the competence the AI domain examines.

## Eligibility, and how to count your three years

The entry requirement is around three years of professional experience in any field. There is no degree requirement and no requirement to have worked in project controls specifically.

Count it full-time-equivalent rather than in calendar years. Part-time work counts pro rata, so two years at half time contributes roughly one year.

Concurrent roles count once, self-employment and contracting count provided you can describe and evidence the work, and a career break does not reset the clock.

Eligibility is confirmed at application, and PCI may verify on a sample or risk basis. Keep a short file of role titles, dates, a one-line description of what you actually did, and someone who could confirm it.

Meeting the requirement means you may be assessed. It says nothing about whether you would pass today, which is a separate and more useful question.

## How to prepare without wasting months

Baseline before you study. Attempt a set of scenario questions cold, before opening any material, and treat the result as a map of where your time should go rather than a verdict.

Work one domain at a time, then practise on that domain immediately while the material is fresh. Reading converts into judgement only when you are forced to choose an answer.

Short, regular sessions beat occasional marathons, because scenario judgement builds through repetition. Finish with full-length timed mocks sat in one block, so pace holds no surprises.

Certuvo is PCI's official platform for preparation and study, and candidates may prepare however they wish. No preparation of any kind changes the certification decision, which is exactly why the credential is worth holding.

## What PCL-AI does not claim

PCI is not accredited by ANAB, IAS or any ISO/IEC accreditation body. The scheme is designed with reference to ISO/IEC 17024 personnel-certification principles, and the current status is published rather than implied. [The certifying body behind the credential](https://projectcontrolsinstitute.org/project-controls-institute) sets out its legal form, what it publishes and what it refuses to claim.

No pass rates, holder numbers or salary outcomes are published, because a founding-stage body that published those would be inventing them.

One scope note worth stating plainly: **15,613 machine calculation checks run and pass across the PFL-AI and PML-AI material, and PCL-AI has no equivalent suite today**. The figure is only true with that scope attached, and quoting it for PCL-AI would be false.

## Frequently asked questions

**How hard is the PCL-AI examination?**
Hard in a specific way rather than a general one. The mechanics are ordinary for an experienced practitioner, but the items ask you to choose the best action in a realistic situation, often where two options are defensible. Most candidates find the difficulty sits in the half of the discipline they do not use monthly, usually project accounting for planners and schedule logic for cost engineers.

**Do I need to know accounting to pass?**
You need to understand what your numbers do to reported results, which is not the same as being an accountant. The Body of Knowledge starts from the accounting foundations rather than assuming them, covering how progress becomes revenue, how a forecast can create a provision, and where cut-off decisions are made. That is the crossing the credential exists to examine.

**Is there a fixed pass rate?**
No. The examination is criterion-referenced, not graded on a curve, so there is no quota and no fixed proportion who succeed. Everyone who demonstrates the required standard passes. The cut score itself is set through a modified-Angoff standard-setting study before the examination goes live, in line with ISO/IEC 17024 principles.

**How long do I have to sit the exam once I have paid?**
The examination fee opens a 12-month scheduling window from the payment date, and the deadline appears in your confirmation and dashboard. There is no advantage to booking early beyond securing a preferred slot, so book when your timed mocks are consistently comfortable rather than when the window makes you.

**Can I sit it from home?**
Yes. The examination is delivered online under remote proctoring, or at a test centre if you prefer. Check-in verifies identity and, for remote sittings, your environment. Test your device, camera, microphone and connection in the room you will actually use, at the time of day you will sit.

**Does PCL-AI expire?**
It is maintained rather than permanent. Certification is kept current through a CPD cycle and recertification, including currency in the governed use of AI. That requirement exists because the AI content in particular dates quickly, and a credential that never expires stops being evidence of anything after a few years.
