# AI project controls certification: what it examines

An AI project controls certification examines the controls discipline — cost, schedule, forecasting, commercial and project accounting — with the governed use of AI examined as competence in its own right, not as a bolted-on module. PCI's PCL-AI is one: 13 domains, 61 knowledge areas, a scenario-based examination, published at USD 500 with a current 30% discount to USD 350.

The category is new enough that the label is being used loosely. This page sets out what the words should mean before it sets out what PCI charges.

## What separates an AI project controls certification from an AI course

Three products are being sold under overlapping names, and they answer different questions.

| What it is | What it examines | What it does not tell an employer |
|---|---|---|
| Generic AI or prompting certificate | Tool fluency, prompt patterns, sometimes a platform's own features | Whether you can produce or defend a project forecast |
| Classic project controls or cost engineering credential | Estimating, cost control, earned value, scheduling, claims | Whether you can judge an AI-generated output before signing it |
| AI-era controls credential | The full controls discipline, with governed AI assessed as a competency | Nothing about tool-specific button-clicking, which dates within a year |

The third only earns its name if the AI content is assessed as judgement rather than recall. "Name three benefits of machine learning" is a quiz question. "This model forecasts a completion cost 12% below the bottom-up estimate; decide whether you would sign the report" is an examination question.

## What PCL-AI covers

The PCI AI Project Controls Leader (PCL-AI) Body of Knowledge holds 13 domains and 61 knowledge areas, proportioned 40/40/20 across project accounting and finance, project management principles, and governed AI.

The finance group runs from double-entry mechanics and project cost coding through revenue from contracts, budgeting and forecasting, to variance analysis and reporting by exception. The delivery group covers cost control, earned value, contracts and commercial management, lifecycle and gates, agile and hybrid delivery, scheduling, business process cycles and quantified risk.

The AI domain covers concepts, data, prompting, tooling, applied workflows, governance and capability across the whole controls lifecycle. Its task statements include evaluating outputs with golden sets and precision and recall, and pricing the review step that evaluation implies.

That last point is the one that distinguishes an examinable AI competency from an opinion about AI. A candidate who can compute precision and recall on a flagged transaction set, and say what the review time costs, has demonstrated something an employer can use.

## What it costs

PCI publishes its fees in US dollars, paid as a single one-time payment, with no instalment option. These are the figures at the time of writing.

| Line | Published fee | Note |
|---|---|---|
| PCL-AI certification examination | USD 500, currently discounted 30% to **USD 350** | Separate from membership |
| Student membership / registration | USD 99 a year, currently discounted 50% to **USD 49.50** | Portal, learning pathway, member resources |
| Membership + exam bundle | **USD 399.50** | The two discounted figures added: 49.50 + 350 |
| Recertification / renewal | **USD 99** per three-year cycle | Keeps membership and access active |
| Exam retake | To be confirmed | Displayed before any retake booking is completed |

The arithmetic over six years, on those published figures, is the bundle at 399.50 plus one renewal at 99 at the start of the second three-year cycle: **USD 498.50**. Preparation is on top of that and is the line most people underestimate, because it is measured in hours rather than dollars.

Two honest caveats. Exam payment buys registration access, not a credential — certification is awarded only after eligibility, assessment and verification. And membership is not itself a certification, which is a distinction some bodies blur and this one should not.

## Who it suits

The credential is aimed at people who are already accountable for a number, or about to be.

**It suits** planning engineers, schedulers, cost engineers and cost controllers who want the finance half of the job examined; controls managers and PMO leads who sign off reports; project finance professionals who need to read a schedule; and delivery managers who have inherited a controls team.

**It suits less well** anyone looking for a software qualification. Nothing here certifies a tool. If your goal is a Primavera P6 or a platform badge, buy that instead and come back for this when you are being asked to defend forecasts rather than produce them.

**It does not suit** a candidate looking to shortcut experience. Eligibility is three years of professional experience in any field, and the examination is scenario-based rather than definitional, which rewards people who have had to explain a variance to someone unhappy about it.

## How the assessment works

The examination is scenario-based multiple choice, single best answer, delivered under proctoring either online or at a test centre. Scenario items ask for a decision and its justification rather than a definition.

Recertification runs on a three-year CPD cycle with a mandatory AI-currency component. That component exists because the governed-AI content dates faster than anything else in the syllabus, and a credential that ignores this ages badly.

Preparation is available through Certuvo, PCI's official platform for preparation and study, and through the published Body of Knowledge, which is free to read. Candidates newer to the discipline can come in through the PCI Foundation pathway instead of applying directly.

## What to ask any certifying body before you pay

The questions below are not about PCI. They are the ones worth asking of any credential in this category, including this one.

**Is the syllabus published in full, free, before you pay?** A body that will only show you the domains after purchase is selling access, not a standard.

**Is the AI content assessed as judgement?** Ask for a sample item. If the sample is a definition question, the AI competency is decorative.

**Who sets the pass mark, and how?** A defensible pass mark comes from a formal standard-setting study, not a round percentage chosen because it sounds rigorous.

**Is the body that trains you also the body that decides your result?** Separation between preparation and certification decisions is a structural safeguard, and you should be able to see it stated.

**What exactly is claimed about recognition?** PCI is an independent certifying body and claims no accreditation, endorsement, affiliation or equivalence with any other organisation. Any body making a stronger claim should be able to name the accreditor and the scope of the accreditation.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need to know how to build a model to pass?**
No. The AI content examines governed use: judging whether an output can be relied on, evaluating it against known outcomes, and taking accountability for the decision that follows. Being able to compute precision and recall on a flagged set matters; being able to train a model does not.

**Is three years' experience really enough?**
Three years in any field is the eligibility bar, not the preparation standard. Candidates from an accounting background usually find the scheduling and earned value material harder, and engineers usually find revenue recognition and cut-off harder. The gap is predictable and it is the point of the credential.

**How long does preparation take?**
It depends entirely on which half of the discipline you already have. Map yourself honestly against the domain task statements, weight your effort towards the group where you cannot show recent work, and treat the published proportions as guidance on where the material concentrates.

**What is the difference between PCL-AI, PFL-AI and PML-AI?**
PCL-AI (13 domains, 61 knowledge areas) certifies the integrated controls discipline. PFL-AI, the PCI AI Project Finance Leader (16 domains, 61 knowledge areas), sits on the finance side. PML-AI, the PCI Project Management Leader – AI (16 domains, 63 knowledge areas), sits on delivery leadership. Choose by the seat you occupy, not by the title you want.

**Will this qualification date as the tools change?**
The tool-specific parts would, which is why they are not examined. Method, evaluation and accountability outlast any particular product: how to score an output against known results, how to price a review step, and who carries the decision. The three-year recertification cycle carries a mandatory AI-currency component precisely because the parts that do move need refreshing.

**Is a credential worth it if my employer will not pay?**
Judge it as you would any purchase: the published fee, the preparation hours at your own rate, and what changes in your work if you pass. Nobody can promise you an outcome, and any body that does is telling you something about itself.
