# Project controls course online: what you actually get

A project controls course online teaches planning, estimating, cost control, earned value, risk and reporting through recorded modules, live sessions or a mix of both. What you actually get depends on one thing: whether someone marks your work. Video you watch alone teaches awareness; exercises somebody corrects teach method.

The word "course" covers both, and the price rarely tells you which one you are buying.

## What formats does a project controls course online come in?

Four delivery models are on sale, and they fail in different ways.

| Format | What it can teach | What it cannot | Main failure mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| Self-paced video library | Vocabulary, formulas, tool menus, the shape of the discipline | Judgement, because nothing you produce is challenged | Enrolment without completion; the library sits unwatched |
| Live online cohort | Method, through exercises worked in front of a tutor and other candidates | Site reality and commercial pressure | Fixed times that lose against a real project week |
| Blended: live sessions plus recorded modules | Both, if the recorded part is preparation and the live part is practice | Anything the marking scheme does not cover | The live part quietly becomes a lecture with a chat window |
| Employer in-house, delivered remotely | Your own procedures, your own cost breakdown, your own data | Anything outside how your organisation already works | Habits get taught as principles |

The strongest online arrangement for a working practitioner is recorded theory plus live marked exercises. You cover the reading at 6am, and you spend the scheduled hour being corrected.

Ask one question before enrolling: who reads what I produce, and what do they write on it. A provider who cannot name the marker is selling a video library with a syllabus attached.

The module-by-module standard to hold any provider against, online or otherwise, is set out in [how to judge a course syllabus](https://projectcontrolsinstitute.org/project-controls-course).

## What the fee should itemise before you pay

Course pricing hides more than it shows. A fee quoted as one number should still break into these lines when you ask.

| Line | What it buys | The question that exposes it |
|---|---|---|
| Tuition | Contact hours, live or recorded | How many hours are live, and with whom? |
| Materials | Notes, worked examples, templates | Do I keep them after access ends? |
| Software access | A licence, or a demonstration | Which product, which edition, for how long? |
| Assessment | Marking of your work by a person | Who marks it, and against what scheme? |
| Access period | How long the platform stays open | What happens at month 13? |
| Certification | Nothing, usually | Is any independent examination included, or a certificate of attendance? |

That last line is where most confusion sits. A course fee and a certification fee are different transactions with different sellers, and a body that sells both should be able to show you they are decided separately.

PCI keeps them apart. At the time of writing, its published fees list the PCL-AI certification examination at USD 500 with a 30% discount currently applied, bringing it to USD 350, and student membership at USD 99 a year with a 50% discount applied, bringing it to USD 49.50. Fees change, and the figure that binds is the one shown at checkout.

Preparation is separate again. The [PCL-AI Body of Knowledge](https://projectcontrolsinstitute.org/body-of-knowledge) is published so you can study from it without buying anything, and Certuvo is PCI's official preparation platform, offering scenario practice and full mock examinations rather than classroom hours.

## The exercise that proves an online course teaches method

Any provider can talk about the critical path. Here is the level of arithmetic a competent online course makes you do in week two, unaided.

A piling package has seven activities. A, site set-up, 5 days, no predecessor. B, survey and setting out, 4 days, after A. C, mobilise the rig, 6 days, after A. D, test pile, 8 days, after B and C. E, reinforcement delivery, 12 days, after A. F, production piling, 20 days, after D and E. G, pile caps, 10 days, after F.

**Forward pass**, early start to early finish. A runs 0 to 5. B runs 5 to 9.

C runs 5 to 11. E runs 5 to 17. D cannot start until both B and C are done, so D runs 11 to 19.

F waits for D and E, so F runs 19 to 39. G runs 39 to 49.

**Project duration: 49 days.**

**Backward pass**, late finish to late start, working from 49. G is 39 to 49. F is 19 to 39. D is 11 to 19. E is 7 to 19. C is 5 to 11. B is 7 to 11. A is 0 to 5.

**Total float** is late start minus early start. A, C, D, F and G all return zero, so the critical path is A–C–D–F–G: 5 + 6 + 8 + 20 + 10 = **49 days**. B and E each carry 2 days of float.

Now the part that separates a course from a video. Reinforcement delivery, activity E, slips 3 days. It had 2 days of float, so the project slips 1 day and E has just joined the critical path.

A course that stops at "the critical path is the longest path" has taught you a definition. One that makes you find the float, spend it, and report the consequence has taught you the job.

## How the examination works online, if you go that far

Certification is a separate decision from any course, and it is worth knowing the mechanics before you commit to a study route.

PCI's examination is scenario-based and sits online under remote proctoring or at an approved centre. Identity is verified with government-recognised photo ID before you start, you sit in a private space, and only expressly permitted materials are allowed.

Entry is three years of professional experience in any field. There is no degree requirement, and the experience does not have to be in project controls, which is why people arrive from quantity surveying, accounts payable and site engineering. Reasonable adjustments are available on request.

What the decision itself is worth, and how it differs from a certificate of attendance, is covered in [what a certification actually proves](https://projectcontrolsinstitute.org/project-controls-certification).

## The three entry routes

| Route | Fee | Examination | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | Paid | Yes | The examined credential, earned |
| Founding | Free, by invitation code during the founding stage | Yes | The examined credential, earned |
| Honorary | Free | No | Honorary Fellow (PCI), which is not an examined credential |

The distinction on the third row is deliberate and worth reading twice. A founding code waives fees, not the examination. An honorary fellowship is a recognition, and it does not make anyone a certified professional.

## What an online course cannot give you

Reps. Nobody is competent at accruals until they have got one wrong at a live month-end and explained it to a commercial manager who is already annoyed.

A credential. A course certificate records that you attended and that the organisation which took your money was satisfied. A certification records that an independent body examined you against a published standard.

Accreditation, either. PCI is not currently accredited by ANAB, IAS or any other ISO/IEC 17024 accreditation body; its scheme is developed with reference to ISO/IEC 17024 principles, and that is stated here rather than blurred, because a body that is vague about its own status has no business assessing anyone else.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is an online project controls course as good as a classroom one?**
For method, yes, and often better, because you can replay the modules you found hard. Classroom keeps two advantages: the questions other people ask, and the discomfort of working an exercise in front of a room. A live online cohort with marked work recovers most of both, as long as it is genuinely live rather than a recording with a chat box.

**How long should an online course take?**
Long enough to produce artefacts. A working practitioner patching two or three gaps needs 40 to 60 hours spread over six to ten weeks. A beginner covering the full monthly cycle needs a structured programme with sequenced marking, and any provider promising full competence in a weekend is selling awareness.

**Do I need a course before sitting a certification examination?**
No. PCI's requirement is competence against the published standard, and how you get there is your business: self-study from the Body of Knowledge, employer coaching, a competitor's course, or a hard year on a difficult project. The certification decision is kept separate from preparation, so no trainer can influence the result.

**Will the course teach Primavera P6?**
Some do, and a licence for the study period is worth asking about explicitly. Learn scheduling method first and a tool second, because employers train tools and rarely train method. A syllabus that lists menus instead of competencies is a software tutorial wearing a controls title.

**Does an online course count towards CPD?**
It can, once you hold a credential. PCI maintains certification over a defined recertification cycle with a required AI-currency component, records are logged by the professional and audited on a sample basis, and the specific hours and cycle rules are published with the recertification requirements. Keep your certificates and your notes either way.
