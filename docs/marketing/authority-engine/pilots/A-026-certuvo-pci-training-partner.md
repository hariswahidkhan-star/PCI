## 9–14. Article (~1,300 words)

Two organisations, one candidate journey, and a boundary that exists for a reason. This page draws
the line precisely, because getting it wrong is how people end up believing they bought something
they did not.

## The one-sentence version

**PCI sets the standard; Certuvo is where you meet it.**

That is PCI's own framing, and the division behind it is strict:

| | PCI (Project Controls Institute) | Certuvo |
|---|---|---|
| Owns the body of knowledge | ✅ | ❌ |
| Writes and owns the examination | ✅ | ❌ |
| Decides eligibility | ✅ | ❌ |
| Marks the examination and decides certification | ✅ | ❌ |
| Issues and maintains the credential | ✅ | ❌ |
| Handles appeals, revocation and verification | ✅ | ❌ |
| Provides practice questions and mock examinations | ❌ | ✅ |
| Provides study tools, progress tracking, AI coaching | ❌ | ✅ |

Certuvo is described by PCI as its **official partner** and *"official platform for exam preparation
and study"*. It is **not** the certification body, does not award the credential, and controls no
part of the certification decision. If any material you encounter suggests otherwise, that material
is wrong.

## Why the separation exists

A body that writes your examination, sells you the course, and then marks your paper has a
commercial interest in how often you sit it. PCI's answer to the obvious question is published:

> *"Does PCI also sell exam preparation? No. PCI develops and owns the standard and the examination.
> Preparation and training are provided separately by Certuvo, our official partner. This separation
> supports the impartiality expected of a body developed with reference to ISO/IEC 17024
> personnel-certification principles."*

Note the careful phrasing at the end — *developed with reference to* those principles. PCI is not
currently accredited by ANAB, IAS or any ISO/IEC 17024 accreditation body, and does not claim to be.
The separation is a design decision made in that direction, not a certificate someone issued.

## What Certuvo actually provides

The mandated description, which appears verbatim wherever access is presented:

> *"Certuvo is an external practice platform. All practice questions, mock examinations, study
> tools, AI coaching, progress tracking and learning activities are available directly within
> Certuvo."*

Two words to hold onto: **external** and **practice**. External, because it is a separate platform
with its own account. Practice, because that is the category — you are rehearsing judgement against
scenarios, not being taught a syllabus you will then be quizzed on verbatim.

The AI coach is worth a specific note, because expectations run ahead of it. It coaches; it does not
compute. In PCI's own labs the equivalent rule is explicit — the coach never computes numbers, it
critiques method. Expect to be told your approach is wrong. Do not expect to be handed the answer.

## What triggers your access

> ⚠️ **`[VERIFY CERTUVO ACCESS TERM]` — this section is the article's one open question.**

The platform's behaviour today: **a Certuvo account is provisioned automatically once your
membership is active**, and appears shortly after payment settles. In the portal's own words:
*"Your Certuvo practice account is set up automatically once your membership is active. It will
appear here shortly after payment."*

The programme brief this article was commissioned under describes the trigger differently — as
following payment of the applicable **examination** fee. Those are not the same condition, and a
candidate who pays only an examination fee expecting automatic study access would be materially
misled if the brief's version were published while the platform behaves otherwise.

**Until PCI confirms which rule is current, this page states the membership rule and says so
openly.** Nothing about access duration is published here either: no fixed term is configured, and
inventing one would be worse than leaving the question visible.

If your access has not appeared after a settled payment, that is a support question, not a
preparation question — the provisioning is automatic, so an absent account means something failed
rather than something is owed.

## Do you have to use it?

No, and PCI says so directly: *"Do I have to use Certuvo to sit the exam? No. Certuvo is the
official preparation, but candidates may prepare however they wish."*

What no provider can promise, PCI states equally plainly: *"Preparation through Certuvo or any other
provider does not guarantee exam success or certification."* Any course, anywhere, that guarantees
you a pass is either misrepresenting its influence over an examining body or misrepresenting the
examination.

Practical guidance rather than a sales pitch:

- **Use it if** you want the closest available rehearsal of the real scenarios, structured analytics
  telling you which domains are weakest, and mock examinations under something like real conditions.
- **You can skip it if** you already work at the level being assessed daily and prefer to close gaps
  from primary material. Plenty of candidates prepare independently.
- **Either way**, treat practice questions as practice: they rehearse the reasoning, they are not the
  examination's questions.

## A four-week structure that works either way

Whether you use the official platform or not, the shape that works is the same:

| Week | Focus | Test of readiness |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Map the syllabus against what you already do daily | You can name your three weakest domains without guessing |
| 2 | Attack the weakest domain only. Practise, review errors, repeat | Your error rate in that domain halves |
| 3 | Full mock under exam conditions — timed, uninterrupted, no notes | You finish inside the time |
| 4 | Second mock, then review only the reasoning behind wrong answers | You can explain *why* each wrong answer was wrong |

The week-four instruction is the one people skip and the one that matters. Reviewing the correct
answer teaches you a fact. Reviewing your reasoning teaches you the mistake you will otherwise make
again under pressure.

## Student FAQ

**Is Certuvo the certification body?** No. PCI owns the standard, the examination and the
certification decision. Certuvo provides preparation and practice.

**Does Certuvo decide whether I pass?** No. Certification decisions are PCI's alone.

**Do I have to use Certuvo?** No. It is optional.

**When does my Certuvo access appear?** The platform provisions automatically once membership is
active, shortly after payment settles. `[VERIFY CERTUVO ACCESS TERM]` — confirm the current
commercial rule with PCI before relying on this.

**How long does access last?** No fixed term is published. Check the access record itself, which
shows its own validity date.

**Will Certuvo guarantee I pass?** No, and PCI states this explicitly. No provider can.

**My access has not appeared — what do I do?** Contact PCI support; provisioning is automatic, so an
absent account indicates a failure rather than an outstanding requirement.
