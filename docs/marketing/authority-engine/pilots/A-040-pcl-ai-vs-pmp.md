## The honest framing

Comparison articles usually exist to declare a winner. This one does not, because on inspection the
two credentials are answering different questions — and the useful output for a reader with one
budget is a decision rule, not a verdict.

**What is settled (PCI side, verified):** PCL-AI — officially the PCI AI Project Controls Leader —
*"unites project-controls governance, cost, planning, earned value, forecasting and risk with
AI-enabled project controls."* Entry is three years of professional experience in any field, with no
degree requirement. Assessment is scenario-based and sat online under remote proctoring. The
credential is valid three years, then recertification, maintained by a CPD cycle including a
mandatory AI-currency component. Fees: USD 500 standard, currently 30% off at USD 350; membership
USD 99/yr currently USD 49.50; bundle USD 399.50; renewal USD 99 per cycle; retake fee confirmed and
displayed before any retake booking. `[PRICE UNVERIFIED]` pending live check.

PCL-AI maps directly onto the gap — integrative judgement across cost, time and risk is exactly what
the step up requires. ⟨FETCH-INFORMED: whether PMP addresses this particular transition⟩.

**2. Delivery manager moving into a capital-projects environment.**
⟨FETCH-INFORMED comparison⟩. PCI-side note: PML-AI, not PCL-AI, is the leadership credential in the
suite — a reader in this situation may be comparing the wrong PCI credential, and this article should

**3. Candidate whose employer's tender requires a specific named certification.**
Neither article nor either body can override a contractual requirement. If the tender names a

employer-specific requirement.

**4. Career changer with three years in an unrelated field.**
PCL-AI's entry requirement admits them; the scenario-based examination will still be hard without
