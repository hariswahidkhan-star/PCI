## Method

1. Fetch each body's official certification page(s); record title, publisher, direct URL, access
   date into the claims register (`docs/marketing/authority-engine/FACT_DOSSIER.md` §10 format).
2. Capture **total-cost components** (application + exam + membership where applicable + renewal),
   never a single headline fee; note currency and member/non-member split.
3. Capture stated time commitments only where the body itself publishes them; otherwise the
   articles say "varies" (Master Prompt §6).
4. Note geographic/regulated-profession constraints in the body's own words.
5. Re-verify any fetched fact older than 90 days before an article that uses it is published.
6. PCI's defensible-distinction claim ("combines project management/controls + finance + applied
   AI in one certification architecture") is **not to be asserted** until a dated sweep of the
   bodies above (plus live SERP research for direct project-controls+AI credentials) is on file;
   until then articles frame it descriptively ("PCI's architecture spans…", no "only"/"first").

## Fairness contract (binding on every comparison article)

No manufactured winner; each comparison carries owner/purpose, audience, domains and depth,
prerequisites, assessment, preparation relationship, total cost, maintenance, constraints,
who-should-choose-which (including "both" and "neither"), a scenario decision guide, limitations,
and a last-verified date — per Master Prompt §6. "Different purpose" is an acceptable and often
correct conclusion; specialised credentials are acknowledged as deeper in their own domain.
