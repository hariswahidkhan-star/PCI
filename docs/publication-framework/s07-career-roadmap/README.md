# S07 — Career Roadmap

**8 documents · prefix `CAR` · [registry](../00-framework/ASSET-REGISTRY.md#s07--career-roadmap-8--prefix-car)**

Routes in, routes up, and what each rung actually requires.

## The risk this series carries

This is the highest-reach series on LinkedIn and therefore the one most likely to drift into career-
influencer content. It must not. The discipline that keeps it honest is a hard prohibition:

- **No salary figures, ranges, pay growth, demand statistics, vacancy counts or hiring rates.** None. Not
  even illustratively. Market data belongs exclusively to S08, which ships as a methodology precisely
  because the survey has not been run. Cross-reference `SAL-06` instead of estimating.
- **No timelines.** Never state how many years a rung takes. Progression varies by sector, employer and
  project size. Say what must be *demonstrated* instead — that is both more honest and more useful.
- **No implied outcomes.** A PCI credential does not guarantee employment, promotion or pay. PCI is
  *developed with reference to ISO/IEC 17024* and is **not accredited**.

What is left after those three prohibitions is the actual value: specificity about what the work is at
each level and what evidence proves you can do it.

## What each document has to deliver

For every rung: the work, the artefacts you own, the decisions you are trusted with, and **the transition
that genuinely blocks people**. For cost, that is moving from producing the report to owning the forecast.
For planning, it is moving from maintaining the programme to defending it. For leadership, it is the shift
from being right to being believed.

`CAR-02` is honest about something the profession rarely says out loud: project controls is usually entered
sideways, not studied for. Treating that as normal rather than as a deficiency is what makes the document
useful to the engineer, quantity surveyor, accountant or site supervisor reading it.

## Cautions specific to this series

- `CAR-07` (portfolio of evidence) must teach **proper anonymisation**. A candidate who describes their
  work in a way that breaches confidentiality has been badly advised by us.
- `CAR-06` should be specific about what genuinely transfers between sectors and what has to be
  relearned — vague reassurance helps nobody make a decision.
- Never invent company names, real projects or quotations.

## Where this meets the rest of the framework

The competency framework (S03) defines what each level requires; this series describes the career that
moves through those levels. Where a document is tempted to define a competency, it links to `CMP-02`
instead.
