# S05 — Certification Handbook

**8 documents · prefix `CER` · [registry](../00-framework/ASSET-REGISTRY.md#s05--certification-handbook-8--prefix-cer)**

The candidate-facing contract: everything someone must know before they book.

## What makes this series different

A handbook is read by people who are about to spend money and time, and who will feel deceived by any
omission they discover later. So the standard here is not accuracy alone — it is **the absence of
unpleasant surprises**.

Two things follow from that. `CER-04` states plainly that the **honorary route requires no examination**,
because a reader who works that out afterwards will distrust everything else. And `CER-02` states that
**PCL-AI is the only credential with a published examination blueprint** — PFL-AI and PML-AI have none yet.

## The numbers, and which ones are real

Confirmed and safe to state: 90-minute duration, four-option single-best-answer items, three-year
validity, opens 15 minutes early with a 30-minute grace period, 365-day scheduling window, free reschedule
up to 72 hours before with a maximum of three, bookings lock 24 hours before, immediate result with domain
breakdown, seven interface languages, three years' professional experience in any field with no degree
requirement.

Not safe to state, and each is a `[CONFIRM: …]`:

| Fact | Why |
|---|---|
| **Examination fee** | Platform seeds **USD 500**; the legacy candidate pack states **USD 350**. Do not pick one — record the conflict. |
| **Number of items** | No count exists anywhere. A job-task analysis will set it. |
| **Retake fee** | Configured as inactive; confirmed before booking. |
| **CPD required hours** | 30 per three-year cycle is a portal *target*, not a published requirement. The binding rule ships with the recertification rules. |
| **Pass mark** | 65 % is configured; the definitive standard comes from a modified-Angoff study. State both. |

## Cautions specific to this series

- Never imply accreditation, government recognition or guaranteed employment. PCI is *developed with
  reference to ISO/IEC 17024* and is **not accredited** by ANAB, IAS or any accreditation body. State the
  founding-stage position plainly rather than hoping the reader does not ask.
- Never conflate membership grades (SPCI/APCI/MPCI/FPCI) with certification.
- `CER-06` describes proctoring honestly, including that the kiosk client is user-space and **cannot block
  Ctrl+Alt+Del**, and that a *held* result under integrity review shows no score or pass/fail until it is
  resolved.
- Facts must agree with the candidate pack in `docs/publications/` — which still carries the retired
  `PCP-AI` naming. Write **PCL-AI**; flag the legacy file rather than editing it.
- This series routes through the platform's `legal_review_required` state before approval
  (`PLATFORM-INTEGRATION.md` §2.1), and supersedes `docs/downloads/candidate-handbook.md` only once
  approved (§4).

## Gating

`CER-01` is the one gated document in this series. The other seven are open — a candidate should not have
to surrender an email address to find out whether they are eligible.
