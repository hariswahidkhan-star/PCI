### 1.1 Retired codes — never use these

`PCP-AI` · `PFIP` · `PFIP-AI` · `CPMD` · `CPMD-AI` · `PDL-AI`, and the retired name
*"Certified Project Controls Professional — AI"*.

**This matters more than it looks.** The Body of Knowledge in `docs/bok/`, the candidate pack in
`docs/publications/` and much of `docs/downloads/` were authored under `PCP-AI` and still carry that name
internally. **PCP-AI became PCL-AI.** When any of the 100 documents draws on that material, it uses
**PCL-AI**. Do not propagate the retired code, and do not "correct" the legacy files as part of authoring —
flag them instead.

### 1.2 Naming rules

- Identifiers are stored and written **clean: no ™ or ® anywhere in PCI branding.**
- Third-party marks are acknowledged once, on a copyright page — not inline.
- British English throughout: *enrolment, organisation, programme, recognised, modelling, behaviour*.

PCI is **developed with reference to ISO/IEC 17024** personnel-certification principles. PCI is **not
accredited** by ANAB, IAS or any other ISO/IEC 17024 accreditation body.

Never write, imply or allow a reader to infer: accreditation, ISO certification, government recognition,
university equivalence, guaranteed employment or salary outcomes. Where status is relevant, state it
plainly. The house formulation is *"founding-stage"*, and the standard footer stamp is:

> Founding-stage document · Version 1.0 — effective date to be confirmed · Reviewed under PCI governance.

### 4.4 Eligibility

**Three years of professional experience, in any field.** No degree requirement. Full-time-equivalent;
part-time counts pro rata; concurrent roles count once; self-employment counts; career breaks do not reset
the clock. Verified on a sample or risk basis.

### 4.5 Recertification

Three-year cycle with a **mandatory AI-currency component**. In the platform, the CPD category satisfying
it is literally named **"AI currency"**. Only approved CPD entries within the current cycle count. CPD
categories: structured learning · learning through work · contribution to the profession · formal study.

## 5. Application routes

Public framing is **three routes into PCI**: **Standard** · **Founding member** · **Honorary Fellow (PCI)**.

Platform routes: `standard`, `founding`, `honorary` (no examination required), `sponsored`,
`complimentary`, `waived_full`, `waived_partial`, `test` (non-public).

The honorary route does **not** require an examination — when writing about routes, be explicit about that,
because a reader who assumes otherwise will feel misled later.

post. A credential is earned by examination; a grade is a membership standing.

## 9. Known inconsistencies in the source material

Record these; do not silently resolve them, and do not repeat them.

1. **Credential level.** The database records all three credentials at level **Leader**; the public
   `certification.html` renders "Level: Professional". → Use **Leader**, and treat the competency-scale
   word "professional" (S03) as a distinct concept, clearly labelled.
2. **Exam fee.** USD 500 (platform) vs USD 350 (legacy pack). → `[CONFIRM: …]`, per §4.3.
3. **Domain group names.** The legacy pack uses *A. Financial Reporting & Accounting / B. Project
   Management, Controls & Delivery / C. AI for Project Controls*; the current blueprint uses *Project
   accounting & finance / Project management principles / AI knowledge & practical approach*. → Use the
   **current blueprint names**.
4. **Retired naming throughout `docs/bok/`, `docs/publications/`, parts of `docs/downloads/`.** → Write
   PCL-AI; flag the legacy file rather than editing it.
