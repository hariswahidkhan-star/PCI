# Publishing Calendar

Fifty weeks, one hundred documents, two per week. Weeks 51 and 52 publish nothing new — they are the
survey field window and the annual review.

The sequence is not arbitrary. It is ordered so that the Institute earns the right to make each claim
before it makes it: teaching first, then the standard behind the teaching, then the credential built on
the standard, then the profession the credential serves.

---

## The argument the sequence makes

| Quarter | Weeks | Theme | What it establishes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Q1** | 1–13 | **The standard exists** | We can teach the work, with arithmetic, better than the alternatives |
| **Q2** | 14–26 | **AI, governed** | We have a defensible position on the thing everyone is confused about — and a competency framework behind it |
| **Q3** | 27–39 | **How the credential is built** | Radical transparency: ethics, blueprint, standard setting, handbook |
| **Q4** | 40–50 | **The profession** | Career reality, and an honest market instrument rather than invented numbers |

Q1 leads with best-practice guides paired to their templates because that pairing is the strongest thing
the programme has: a method, then the instrument that runs it. Nobody has to trust the Institute to
benefit from week two.

Certification content (Q3) deliberately comes **after** thirty-nine weeks of teaching. A body that has
taught for nine months and then explains its examination reads very differently from one that opens with
an enrolment pitch.

## Weeks 51–52 — Field and review

No new documents.

- **Week 51** — the salary and skills survey opens, using `SAL-02`. The instrument has been public for
  three weeks by this point, which is itself the methodological argument: anyone can inspect the questions
  before answering them.
- **Week 52** — annual review. Every document's status re-checked, corrections consolidated, the year's
  reader corrections acknowledged publicly, and the following year's calendar published.

## Sequencing rules (for when this calendar is edited)

1. **A guide ships before or with its template, never after.** The method earns the instrument.
2. **Nothing from S05 (certification) or S08 (salary) ships in Q1.** The Institute teaches for a quarter
   before it mentions its own examination, and never publishes a market instrument before it has
   published its methodology.
3. **`SAL-05` never ships before `SAL-01`.** A report template released without its methodology invites
   exactly the misreading the series exists to prevent.
4. **`EXB-08` (sample items) never ships before `EXB-01` (the blueprint).** Sample items without the
   syllabus behind them read as a quiz.
5. **`AIG-08` (governing AI) ships before `AIG-11` (evaluating tools).** Governance first, procurement
   second — the reverse order is how organisations end up governing a tool they already bought.
6. **No more than two consecutive weeks from the same series.** The feed is a mixture or it is a
   monologue.
7. **A document with unresolved `[CONFIRM: …]` placeholders cannot be scheduled.** It has not passed the
   gate in `GOVERNANCE-AND-REVIEW.md` §3, and the calendar is not a way around that.
