# S01 — Body of Knowledge: Executive Summary

**6 documents · prefix `PCB` · [registry](../00-framework/ASSET-REGISTRY.md#s01--body-of-knowledge-executive-summary-6--prefix-pcb)**

The executive-summary layer over the thirteen-domain Body of Knowledge in `docs/bok/`.

## What makes this series different

**The Body of Knowledge is never republished here.** `docs/bok/` is roughly a megabyte of manuscript plus a
PDF; almost nobody reads it end to end, and it is not the artefact that travels. This series is the layer
that does travel — the summary a director finishes, the domain map an employer pins up, and the argument
for why the weighting is what it is.

The relationship runs one way: the Body of Knowledge is the source of truth, and a summary that
contradicts it is wrong by definition.

## Cautions specific to this series

- `docs/bok/` was authored under the **retired** `PCP-AI` code. The credential is **PCL-AI**. Do not
  propagate the old name, and do not edit the legacy files to fix it — flag them instead.
- The 40/40/20 weighting and the 13-domain / 61-knowledge-area structure are fixed facts
  (`CANONICAL-FACTS.md` §3). Nothing in this series may restate them differently.
- `PCB-03` defends the weighting as an intellectual position. It is the document most likely to be argued
  with in public, so its reasoning must stand without appeal to authority.

## Reading order

`PCB-01` establishes the whole claim and is the flagship. `PCB-02` is its visual companion. `PCB-03`
supplies the argument behind the structure, `PCB-04` states the knowledge claim as propositions, `PCB-05`
shows the method that produced it, and `PCB-06` turns all of it into something an employer can use.
