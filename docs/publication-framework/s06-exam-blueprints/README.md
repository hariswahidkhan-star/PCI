# S06 — Exam Blueprint

**8 documents · prefix `EXB` · [registry](../00-framework/ASSET-REGISTRY.md#s06--exam-blueprint-8--prefix-exb)**

Radical transparency about assessment design. This is the Institute's strongest credibility asset,
because almost nobody publishes it.

## PCL-AI only

**No examination blueprint exists yet for PFL-AI or PML-AI** — no weightings, no item counts. Every
document in this series says so rather than implying coverage it does not have. A reader who assumes the
suite is uniformly specified and later discovers otherwise has been misled, even if no sentence was
technically false.

## The three facts this series must never get wrong

1. **There is no item count.** None exists anywhere in the platform. Item counts *will be confirmed by a
   formal job-task analysis*. Stating a number — even a plausible one, even hedged — is the single most
   likely error in this series and a hard failure at gate.
2. **The pass mark is two facts, not one.** 65 % is the configured mark; the definitive standard *will be
   set by a modified-Angoff standard-setting study*. Say both, every time.
3. **Scoring is criterion-referenced, not graded on a curve.** Candidates are measured against the
   standard, not against each other.

Safe to state: 90 minutes, four-option multiple choice with a single best answer, Recall/Application/
Analysis cognitive levels weighted toward application, no formula sheet provided in the examination,
immediate result with domain breakdown, three-year validity, 13 domains and 61 knowledge areas weighted
40/40/20.

Use the **current** group names — *Project accounting & finance*, *Project management principles*,
*AI knowledge & practical approach* — not the legacy A/B/C naming in `docs/publications/`.

## Cautions specific to this series

- Sample items in `EXB-08` are **original study items**, kept separate from the live bank, with every
  distractor explained and every calculation verified. Never reproduce live examination content.
- `EXB-08` never ships before `EXB-01`. Sample items without the syllabus behind them read as a quiz
  (`PUBLISHING-CALENDAR.md` sequencing rule 4).
- Never invent psychometric statistics, pass rates or candidate volumes.
- This series carries quasi-regulatory weight and routes through the platform's `legal_review_required`
  state before approval — see `PLATFORM-INTEGRATION.md` §2.1.

## Why publish this at all

Because a body that explains how its pass mark will be set, and admits what it has not yet fixed, is
making a claim that cannot be faked. `EXB-06` — where the blueprint gets its authority — is the document
that carries that argument.
