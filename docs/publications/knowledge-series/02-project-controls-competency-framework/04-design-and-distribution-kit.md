# Design and Distribution Kit — Knowledge Series 02

**Publication:** *Show Your Work — The Project Controls Competency Framework*
**Institution:** PCI AI · Project Controls Institute Global, Inc.

---

## 1. Suggested cover page

**Concept — "the artefact, not the adjective."**

The cover carries one visual idea: a hand-drawn margin tick beside a worked line. In the lower third, three short gold rules sit stacked and left-aligned — the first solid and complete, the second solid, the third trailing into dots. Beside the third, a small gold check mark.

It reads as a worked page that has been checked. That is the entire argument of the publication: competence is a thing produced and verified, not a quality asserted.

| Element | Specification |
|---|---|
| Trim | A4 portrait, 210 × 297 mm, 5 mm bleed |
| Ground | Deep navy `#0B1B2B`, matte |
| Title | *Show Your Work* — serif display, 84 pt, off-white `#F4F1EA`, upper-left |
| Subtitle | "The Project Controls Competency Framework" — sans, 22 pt, 70% opacity |
| Illustration | Three stacked rules with the check mark, gold `#C9A227`, at 62% page height |
| Series mark | "KNOWLEDGE SERIES · 02" — sans, 12 pt, letter-spaced 0.2em, gold, top-right |
| Institution | "PCI AI · PROJECT CONTROLS INSTITUTE GLOBAL" — sans, 11 pt, bottom-left |
| Logo | Bottom-right, single colour, max 24 mm wide |

**Series continuity.** The layout grid, type scale, accent colour and series-mark position are identical to Publication 01. Only the illustration changes. Across a shelf of fourteen, the series should read as one body of work.

**What to avoid.** No photography of people at desks. No graduation imagery. No ladders rendered literally on the cover — the ladder is inside the document and would be a cliché on the front.

## 2. Suggested hero infographic

**Title: "The Competency Matrix — fourteen competencies, five levels, one question"**

A single-page A3 or 1600 × 2000 px asset built around the full 14 × 5 matrix.

**Structure, top to bottom:**

**Band 1 — the question.** "What can this person be trusted to sign?" set large, with the five signing answers running beneath as a horizontal scale: *nothing · routine work · own judgement · others' work · the standard*.

**Band 2 — the matrix.** All fourteen competencies as rows, five levels as columns, grouped by the five clusters with cluster bands in alternating tone. Cells carry a condensed descriptor. This is a dense asset by design — density is the credibility signal here, and readers will zoom.

**Band 3 — the failure column.** Set apart to the right of the matrix, in a contrasting tone: the characteristic failure at each level. Visually separated so it reads as a distinct instrument, because it is.

**Band 4 — coverage.** The Coverage Index formula, the four-band scale, and a worked example strip showing the composite case (cost 0.89, risk 0.11) with the annotation "same team".

**Design notes.** This asset must survive being printed A3 and pinned to a wall — that is its actual use. Test legibility at 100% on paper before release. One accent colour. Tabular figures throughout.

## 7. Call to action — education first

**Primary CTA:**

> **Use the framework. It is published in full, including the matrix.**
> Fourteen competencies, five levels, role profiles and the assurance checklist. No registration, no email gate.
> projectcontrolsinstitute.org

**Why it is written this way.** Organisations building an internal competency framework will otherwise assemble one from fragments. Giving the complete matrix away means the Institute's structure becomes the reference implementation — which is worth considerably more than the email addresses a gate would collect.

**Secondary CTA, for the Coverage Index derivative:**
> Compute the Coverage Index for one competency in your own function: people at Level 3 or above, divided by concurrent projects. If it is below 0.5, most of your projects are going without.

**Tertiary CTA, for the Apprenticeship Gap derivative:**
> If you automated your junior production work tomorrow, where would your next generation of judgement come from? We do not think this is a settled question.

**Never use.** Any implication that the credential confers a proficiency level. The framework is explicit that an examination establishes registers 1 and 2 and only partly register 3 — marketing must not contradict the publication's own honesty.

## 10. Editorial compliance checks

- [ ] No claim of accreditation, ISO recognition or government endorsement.
- [ ] No claim about employment, salary, promotion or immigration outcomes.
- [ ] No implication that holding the credential confers a proficiency level.
- [ ] Every statistic carries a named source and year.
- [ ] Every worked figure recomputed and verified.
- [ ] The case study is explicitly labelled an illustrative composite.
- [ ] No client, project or organisation identifiable.
- [ ] Cited academic work (Dreyfus, Ericsson, Miller) described accurately and attributed.
- [ ] The fourteen competencies match the platform's published PCL-AI list exactly.
- [ ] British English. No emojis.
- [ ] The notices block present and unaltered on any PDF asset.
