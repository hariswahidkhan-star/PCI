## 1. Suggested cover page

**Concept — "the plan that becomes uncertain."**

A single hairline rule runs horizontally across the lower third of the cover. For the left two-thirds it is solid gold. At the point of the golden section it breaks into a dotted line and begins to fan into three faint diverging paths that run off the right edge.

That is the entire illustration. It states the publication's argument before a word is read: a plan is certain behind you and a range ahead of you, and the professional's work is at the point where it breaks.

| Element | Specification |
|---|---|
| Trim | A4 portrait, 210 × 297 mm, 5 mm bleed |
| Ground | Deep navy `#0B1B2B`, matte |
| Title | *Knowing Early* — serif display, 84 pt, off-white `#F4F1EA`, upper-left quadrant |
| Subtitle | "An Executive Summary of the Project Controls Body of Knowledge" — sans, 22 pt, 70% opacity, directly beneath |
| Illustration | The breaking rule, gold `#C9A227`, vertically at 62% of page height |
| Series mark | "KNOWLEDGE SERIES · 01" — sans, 12 pt, letter-spaced 0.2em, gold, top-right |
| Institution | "PCI AI · PROJECT CONTROLS INSTITUTE GLOBAL" — sans, 11 pt, letter-spaced, bottom-left |
| Logo | Bottom-right, single colour, no larger than 24 mm wide |
| Edition line | "First edition" — 10 pt, 50% opacity, beneath the institution line |

**What to avoid.** No photography of construction sites, hard hats, handshakes or skylines. No gradient meshes. No stock imagery of any kind. The cover's authority comes from restraint and white space, and every competitor in this category uses the same three photographs.

**Back cover.** Navy ground, the six-conversion hexagon rendered small and centred in gold outline, the institution's URL beneath, and the notices block at 8 pt.

## 2. Suggested hero infographic

**Title: "The Controls Value Chain — where a project loses its warning time"**

A single-page A3 or 1600 × 2000 px asset. This is the publication's signature visual and should be produced to a standard that survives being screenshotted and shared without the document.

**Structure, top to bottom:**

**Band 1 — the loop.** The hexagon: six nodes, clockwise arrows, closed. Each node carries its conversion name and a small latency figure. Nodes 1 and 6 are filled gold; the others are outlined. A dashed rectangle encloses nodes 2, 3 and 4, labelled "where software helps".

**Band 2 — the leak.** Directly beneath each node, a downward-pointing wedge sized to that conversion's characteristic damage, with the failure named: cut-off gap, miscoding, progress by opinion, single-point forecast, orphan variance, baseline drift. The wedges under nodes 1 and 6 are visibly the largest. The asymmetry is the message.

**Band 3 — the equation.** `E = F × T × A` set large and centred, with the three-scenario table beneath it. The 0.36 → 0.38 → 0.65 progression rendered as three bars.

**Band 4 — the footer.** The domain map: thirteen numbered dots arranged around a miniature of the hexagon, colour-coded by competency area, with the 40 / 40 / 20 split shown as a slim stacked bar.

**Design notes.** One accent colour only. Numerals in tabular figures throughout. Every label under nine words. The infographic must be legible at 25% zoom on a phone — test this before release.

## 7. Call to action — education first

**Primary CTA, used on the publication, the carousel and the landing page:**

> **Read the full Body of Knowledge before you consider the credential.**
> Thirteen domains and sixty-one knowledge areas, published openly. No registration, no email gate.
> projectcontrolsinstitute.org

**Why it is written this way.** A gate converts better in the short term and costs more than it earns here. The audience is senior, sceptical and allergic to lead capture. Publishing openly is itself the argument that the framework can withstand scrutiny — and a professional who has read the Body of Knowledge and found it rigorous is a better candidate than one who traded an email address for a PDF.

**Secondary CTA, for the checklist derivative:**
> Run the 31-point Controls Assurance Checklist against one of your own projects. If you score below 20, the constraint is governance, not tooling.

**Tertiary CTA, for the maturity-model derivative:**
> Three questions separate a controls function that reports from one that steers. They are in Section 13.

**Never use on this publication.** Countdown timers, "limited places", "enrol now", discount framing, or any claim of accreditation, recognition or career outcome.

## 10. Editorial compliance checks

Run before any release. Each is a hard gate.

- [ ] No claim of accreditation, ISO recognition or government endorsement anywhere in the asset.
- [ ] No claim about employment, salary, promotion or immigration outcomes.
- [ ] Every statistic carries a named source and year.
- [ ] Every worked figure has been recomputed and verified.
- [ ] The case study is explicitly labelled as an illustrative composite.
- [ ] No client, project or organisation is identifiable.
- [ ] No standard's text is reproduced; all standards are described in the publication's own words.
- [ ] British English throughout.
- [ ] No emojis.
- [ ] The notices block is present and unaltered on any PDF asset.
