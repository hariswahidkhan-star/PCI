## SLIDE 5 — Framework: the six conversions

**Headline**
## A control system performs six conversions.

**List, compact**
1. Reality → Record
2. Record → Structure
3. Structure → Position
4. Position → Forecast
5. Forecast → Decision
6. Decision → Reality

**Visual**
Hexagonal closed loop, six nodes, clockwise arrows. Nodes **1** and **6** in gold.

---

## SLIDE 6 — Where it actually breaks

**Headline**
## Investment goes to 3 and 4.

## Damage comes from 1 and 6.

**Body**
Dashboards and forecasting engines improve the middle of the loop. The two conversions that touch reality — how cost enters the record, and whether a decision lands in the baseline — are the least governed.

**Visual**
The same hexagon, with a dashed box around nodes 2–4 labelled "where software helps", and nodes 1 and 6 pulsing gold outside it.

## SLIDE 7 — Framework: the effectiveness equation

**Headline, oversized, centred**
# E = F × T × A

**Legend**
**Fidelity** — is the number right?
**Timeliness** — does it arrive while the decision is open?
**Authority** — is there a named person who can act?

**Body, one line**
Multiplicative. Not additive.

**Visual**
Three vertical sliders feeding a single product readout.

## SLIDE 9 — The worked case

**Headline**

**Body, tight**

Invoiced cost only → CPI 0.957 → EAC **438.9m**
Accrual booked (+12.4m) → CPI 0.887 → EAC **473.3m**

**Callout in gold**
The 34.4m gap exceeded the entire 21m contingency. The board was told the project was contained.

**Visual**
Two diverging lines from month 6 to month 14, the gap between them shaded gold and labelled.

## SLIDE 11 — The AI position

**Headline**
## AI proposes. The professional disposes.

**Body**
AI changes the cost of analysis. It does not change where accountability sits.

Place each task by one question: **what happens if this is wrong and nobody notices?**

**Ladder, compact**
L0 Prohibited — certifications and legal positions
L2 Analytical — AI detects, human verifies every flag
L4 Automated with assurance — routine, sampled, audited
L5 Autonomous — not permitted for accountable outputs

**Visual**
Vertical ladder, six rungs. L5 struck through in gold.

## Publishing notes

**Post as a document (PDF), not as images.** LinkedIn's document viewer produces higher dwell time than a carousel of image posts, and dwell time is the ranking signal that matters here.

**Slide 8 is the hook slide.** If the post underperforms, test moving slide 8 to position 3 — the counter-intuitive arithmetic is the strongest scroll-stopper in the deck.

**Accessibility.** Provide alt text for each slide in the post body or first comment. Gold on navy passes contrast at the specified weights; do not reduce the accent weight below 500.

**Do not add a certification pitch to any slide.** The deck earns attention by teaching. The credential is discoverable by anyone who wants it.
