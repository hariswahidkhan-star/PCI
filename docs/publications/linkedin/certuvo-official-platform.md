## A note on the framing

This was requested as an "official training partner" announcement. It is published here as
an **official preparation platform** announcement instead, for three reasons — all of which
come from PCI's own published pages rather than from editorial preference.

**1. "Training Partner" is a defined programme for third parties.** `training-partners.html`
sets out three tiers — Registered, Authorized, Premier — that outside organisations apply to
and are assessed against. Certuvo is described site-wide as "PCI's official platform for exam
preparation and study," which is a first-party role. Announcing it inside the partner tiers
would misdescribe Certuvo and devalue the tiers for the organisations genuinely applying.

**2. The Impartiality Policy says the opposite of what a partner announcement implies.** It
states that PCI keeps certification "structurally separate from training," that Certuvo "has
no role in setting the cut score or in any certification decision," and — in terms —
that **"completing its courses confers no advantage."** A partnership announcement carries the
implication that the endorsed training improves your chances. That implication contradicts
the policy.

**3. It is the weaker post.** Every certification body announces training partners; the
posts are interchangeable and nobody reads them. Almost none announce what the relationship
is forbidden to do. Leading with the limits is the version a senior audience finds credible,
and it is the version that survives scrutiny from a candidate, a complainant or an assessor.

**If Certuvo has in fact signed a third-party training-partner agreement** and is being
admitted to one of the three tiers, tell me — that is a different announcement with a
different governance footing, and it would need the tier named and the impartiality wording
adjusted before it goes out.

## Claims deliberately not made

- **No accreditation claim.** PCI is not accredited by ANAB, IAS or any ISO/IEC 17024
  accreditation body; the framework is being developed with reference to ISO/IEC 17024
  principles. The deck therefore states the separation as PCI's own governance commitment and
  never as compliance with, or accreditation against, a standard.
- **No outcome claim.** Nothing suggests that preparing with Certuvo improves the chance of
  passing — the policy says the opposite, and the deck says the opposite.
- **No exclusivity claim.** Certuvo being the official platform does not make it required,
  and slide 6 exists specifically to say so.
- **No pass-rate, enrolment or candidate-volume figures**, since none are published.
