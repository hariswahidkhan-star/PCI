# The PCP-AI Credential — Overview

**Certified Project Controls Professional — AI** · Project Controls Institute

*A concise overview for prospective candidates, employers and training partners.*

---

## Project controls has changed. The credential should too.

Projects are still won or lost on the fundamentals — accurate cost, honest forecasts, a schedule you can
trust, contracts that hold, risk that is understood. What has changed is that a new tool now sits on
every controls professional's desk: **artificial intelligence**. Used well, it drafts reports, spots
anomalies in a cost ledger, and sharpens a forecast. Used carelessly, it invents numbers with total
confidence.

The **Certified Project Controls Professional — AI (PCP-AI)** is built for exactly this moment. It
certifies the classical discipline of project controls **and** the judgement to use AI responsibly — under
one clear principle: **AI proposes; the professional disposes.** The human stays accountable.

## What it certifies

A PCP-AI holder can demonstrate capability across three areas:

- **Financial reporting & accounting (40%)** — the accounting model, IFRS/IAS-based reporting, revenue
  recognition on contracts, cost management and the commercial cycle. Controls professionals who
  understand the numbers behind the numbers.
- **Project management, controls & delivery (40%)** — budgeting and forecasting, performance and variance
  analysis, **earned value management**, scheduling and the critical path, contracts and commercial
  management, the project lifecycle, **agile and adaptive delivery**, risk, and process controls.
- **AI for project controls (20%)** — what AI genuinely is and is not, data quality, working with
  generative AI, the tool landscape, AI applied across the controls lifecycle, and — critically — the
  **governance, ethics and assurance** that keep a human accountable for every output.

Underneath sits the **PCP-AI Body of Knowledge**: thirteen domains, sixty-one Knowledge Areas, developed
to full professional-handbook depth with worked examples, figures and practice questions.

## Why it is different

- **AI is woven through, not bolted on.** Every domain shows where AI helps a specific task and where it
  must be governed — an honest treatment of real capabilities and real limits, not hype.
- **It is quantitative and applied.** Candidates compute earned-value indices, forecasts, variances,
  agile metrics and risk expected values — not just recall definitions.
- **It is examined securely and fairly.** A 90-minute, remotely-proctored examination, a 65% standard,
  immediate results with a domain-by-domain breakdown, and an interface available in seven languages.

## Who should hold it

Cost engineers, planners and schedulers, project and commercial controllers, quantity surveyors, PMO
analysts, and project-facing finance professionals who want a current, recognised credential that
reflects both the discipline and its AI-augmented future.

## The pathway

1. **Study** the Body of Knowledge and the published Content Outline.
2. **Register and pay** the examination fee — this opens a 12-month scheduling window.
3. **Sit** the 90-minute proctored examination, in the browser or the PCI Secure Exam application.
4. **Earn** a verifiable PCP-AI credential, and keep it current with continuing professional development
   on a three-year cycle.
