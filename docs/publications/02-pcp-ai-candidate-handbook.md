# PCP-AI Candidate Handbook

**Certified Project Controls Professional — AI (PCP-AI)** · Project Controls Institute

*Version 1 — published for candidates. This handbook explains what the PCP-AI credential is, how the
examination works, and the policies that govern it. Figures such as fees and continuing-professional-
development requirements are the Institute's current settings and are confirmed on the official PCI
website at the time of registration.*

---

## 1. About the credential

The **PCP-AI** certifies that a project-controls professional can combine three things that modern
projects demand together: sound **financial-reporting and accounting** judgement, the full **project-
management and controls** toolkit (budgeting, earned value, scheduling, contracts, risk and process
controls), and the **governed use of artificial intelligence** in project controls.

The AI dimension is what sets the credential apart, and it is taught and examined honestly. The
governing principle is **"AI proposes; the professional disposes"**: AI can draft, summarise, detect and
forecast, but the certified professional remains accountable for every estimate, commitment, forecast
and decision, and is expected to know where AI helps, where it fails, and how to govern it.

## 2. Who it is for

The credential suits cost engineers, planners and schedulers, project and commercial controllers,
quantity surveyors, PMO analysts, and finance professionals working on or alongside projects who want a
recognised, current qualification that reflects both classical project controls and the responsible
adoption of AI. There are no mandatory prerequisites to sit the examination; candidates should judge
their readiness against the published Content Outline and Body of Knowledge.

## 3. Registration and fees

1. Create a PCI account and complete your profile.
2. Pay the **examination fee** — currently **USD 350** (confirmed at checkout; local pricing and any
   discounts are shown on the website). Payment opens a **12-month window** in which to schedule and sit
   the examination.
3. Provide a valid **government-issued photo ID** whose name matches your registration; identity is
   verified before the examination.

The examination fee covers one sitting. A further sitting (for example, after an unsuccessful attempt)
requires a new examination fee.

## 4. The examination

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Format | Multiple-choice questions, four options, one correct answer |
| Duration | **90 minutes** |
| Pass mark | **65%** |
| Areas | A. Financial Reporting & Accounting (40%) · B. Project Management, Controls & Delivery (40%) · C. AI for Project Controls (20%) |
| Delivery | Online, **remotely proctored** — in the browser or via the **PCI Secure Exam** desktop application |
| Interface languages | English, Arabic (right-to-left), Spanish, French, Korean, Chinese and Russian |
| Aids | An on-screen calculator is provided; no other materials are permitted |
| Result | Issued **immediately** on submission, with a domain-by-domain performance breakdown |

The examination is built from the published Content Outline. Questions span three cognitive levels —
recall, application and analysis — and quantitative areas include numerical items that you compute before
selecting an answer.

## 5. Booking, rescheduling and the window

- **Booking.** Choose a date and time inside your 12-month window. Launch opens **15 minutes before**
  your slot and stays open for a **30-minute grace period**.
- **Rescheduling.** You may reschedule **free of charge up to 72 hours before** your slot, up to **three
  times**. Bookings **lock 24 hours before** the start time.
- **The window cannot be extended.** If the 12-month window lapses without a sitting, a new examination
  fee is required.
- **Missed sittings.** If you do not launch within the grace period the booking is marked missed;
  documented incidents (for example, a genuine technical failure) are considered under the examination
  administration policy — open a support ticket with evidence.

## 6. On the day — readiness, identity and conduct

Before you launch you will complete a short **system readiness check** (camera, microphone, connection
and screen) and an **identity check** against your photo ID. Access to your camera and microphone is
requested only to confirm they work and to support proctoring; the readiness check itself records
nothing.

During the examination:

- Work alone, in a quiet room, on a single screen, with notes and devices put away.
- The secure environment runs in full screen and monitors for leaving the examination window; copy,
  cut and paste are disabled. Losing focus or leaving the window is recorded as an integrity event on
  your attempt.
- The examination **cannot be quit** — submit when you are ready, or it submits automatically when time
  expires. Answers are saved continuously, so a brief disconnection does not lose your work or reset the
  clock; you resume where you left off.

## 7. Results, scoring and the credential

- **Immediate result.** On submission you receive your score, a **pass/fail** outcome against the 65%
  standard, and a **domain-by-domain breakdown** showing where you performed above, at, or below target.
- **Immediate publication is the default.** Proctoring evidence is retained for audit but does not, by
  itself, delay your result. A result is only held back for a genuine **technical-validity** issue (for
  example, a late or invalid submission), and you are told plainly that no pass/fail outcome is shown
  until it is resolved.
- **The credential.** A pass issues a **PCP-AI credential** with a unique credential ID, verifiable
  online by anyone with the ID. Certificates and score reports are downloadable from your account.

## 8. Keeping the credential — recertification and CPD

The PCP-AI credential is valid for a **three-year cycle**. To keep it current you record **continuing
professional development (CPD)** and recertify within the cycle. The current CPD target is set by the
Institute (presently **30 hours per cycle**) and is shown, with your running total, in your account.
Letting a credential lapse suspends its "active" status until recertification requirements are met.

## 9. Accommodations

The Institute supports candidates who need reasonable adjustments (for example, **extra time**, a
**separate setting**, or **assistive technology**). Request an accommodation from your account **before
booking**, describing what you need; requests are treated confidentially and reviewed by the Institute,
and any approved extra time is applied to your examination automatically.

## 10. Integrity, appeals and data

- **Integrity.** The examination is proctored and the item bank is protected. Attempts to gain an unfair
  advantage, to capture or share examination content, or to have another person sit the examination are
  serious breaches and may invalidate a result and the credential.
- **Appeals and casework.** You may raise an appeal or a support case from your account; the Institute
  operates a documented casework process for accommodations, appeals and incidents.
- **Data and privacy.** Personal data, identity documents and proctoring evidence are handled under the
  Institute's privacy policy and retained only as long as needed for certification and audit. See the
  privacy policy on the website for detail.
