# PCP-AI Sample Questions

**Certified Project Controls Professional — AI (PCP-AI)** · Project Controls Institute

*Twelve sample questions illustrating the style, coverage and difficulty of the PCP-AI examination. These
are **study items** drawn from the same blueprint as, but kept separate from, the live examination bank;

## Area A — Financial Reporting & Accounting

**1. [recall]** A project entity reports total assets of $4,200,000 and total liabilities of $1,500,000.
Applying the accounting equation, what is its equity?
A. $5,700,000 · **B. $2,700,000** · C. $1,500,000 · D. $4,200,000
*Equity = Assets − Liabilities = 4,200,000 − 1,500,000 = $2,700,000. Adding the two figures (A) is the
common error.*

**2. [recall]** Under IFRS 15, revenue from a contract with a customer is recognised when (or as):
**A. a performance obligation is satisfied** · B. cash is received · C. the invoice is raised · D. the
financial year ends
*IFRS 15 recognises revenue as control of the promised good or service transfers — i.e. as a performance
obligation is satisfied — not on cash receipt or invoicing.*

**3. [application]** An asset costs $50,000, has a residual value of $5,000 and a useful life of 5 years.
What is the annual straight-line depreciation charge?
A. $10,000 · B. $11,000 · **C. $9,000** · D. $45,000
*(50,000 − 5,000) / 5 = 45,000 / 5 = $9,000. Ignoring residual value gives $10,000 (A).*

**4. [analysis]** Amounts billed to a customer on a contract exceed the revenue recognised to date. In the
statement of financial position this gives rise to a:
A. contract asset · **B. contract liability** · C. trade receivable · D. prepayment
*Billing ahead of recognised revenue creates a contract liability (deferred revenue); recognising ahead
of billing would create a contract asset.*

## Area B — Project Management, Controls & Delivery

**5. [application]** Earned value (EV) is $90,000 and actual cost (AC) is $120,000. What is the cost
performance index (CPI)?
**A. 0.75** · B. 1.33 · C. 0.90 · D. 1.00
*CPI = EV / AC = 90,000 / 120,000 = 0.75 (under-performing on cost). Inverting the ratio gives 1.33 (B).*

**6. [application]** Budget at completion (BAC) is $1,000,000 and the CPI to date is 0.80. Assuming past
cost efficiency continues, what is the estimate at completion?
A. $800,000 · **B. $1,250,000** · C. $1,200,000 · D. $1,000,000
*EAC = BAC / CPI = 1,000,000 / 0.80 = $1,250,000.*

**7. [application]** Budget at completion is $1,000,000, earned value is $400,000 and actual cost is
$500,000. What is the to-complete performance index (TCPI) to finish on the original budget?
A. 0.80 · **B. 1.20** · C. 1.00 · D. 0.83
*TCPI = (BAC − EV) / (BAC − AC) = 600,000 / 500,000 = 1.20 — the efficiency now required on remaining
work.*

**8. [analysis]** An activity on a project network has a total float of zero. This activity is:
A. non-critical · B. a milestone · C. a dummy activity · **D. on the critical path**
*Zero total float means any delay to the activity delays the project — the definition of a critical-path
activity.*

**9. [application]** A risk has a 20% probability of occurring and a $50,000 impact if it does. What is its
expected monetary value (EMV)?
A. $50,000 · B. $250,000 · C. $70,000 · **D. $10,000**
*EMV = probability × impact = 0.20 × 50,000 = $10,000.*

**10. [application]** An agile team completes 24 story points across three sprints. What is its average
velocity?
A. 24 points/sprint · **B. 8 points/sprint** · C. 72 points/sprint · D. 3 points/sprint
*Velocity = points completed / sprints = 24 / 3 = 8 points per sprint.*

## Area C — AI for Project Controls

**11. [recall]** In project controls, the governing principle "AI proposes; the professional disposes"
means that:
**A. the professional remains accountable for the final decision** · B. AI outputs can be accepted as
always correct · C. AI replaces the cost engineer · D. forecasts are issued automatically without review
*The principle keeps a qualified human accountable for every estimate, forecast and decision, however the
output was generated.*

**12. [analysis]** A generative-AI assistant drafts a cost narrative from a project's ledger, but the
ledger is missing an accrual for work done and not yet invoiced. The most likely consequence is that the
AI:
A. corrects the missing accrual automatically · B. refuses to produce a narrative · **C. produces a
fluent but wrong narrative, overstating cost performance** · D. flags the data as incomplete on its own
*AI reasons over the data it is given. An incomplete input yields a confident, well-written, but incorrect
output — which is exactly why the professional must verify the inputs and the result.*
