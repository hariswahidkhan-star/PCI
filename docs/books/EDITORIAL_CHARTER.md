# Editorial Charter — PCI PML-AI and PFL-AI Bodies of Knowledge

**Status:** Phase 0 deliverable · binding on every contributor and agent role
**Owner:** Chief editorial coordinator
**Applies to:** the PML-AI BoK and the PFL-AI BoK, their companion files and student editions

---

## 1. Mission

Produce two completely separate, original, publication-ready professional books — the **PCI Project
Management Leader – AI Body of Knowledge (PML-AI)** and the **PCI Project Finance Leader – AI Body of
Knowledge (PFL-AI)** — each at least **1,200 genuinely useful finished pages** after professional
typesetting, following the validated pattern of the approved PCP-AI Body of Knowledge
(`PCI_BOOK_PATTERN_SPEC.md`) so that PCP-AI, PML-AI and PFL-AI are immediately recognisable as one
professional certification suite.

Portfolio theme, used where it genuinely fits (never as a per-page slogan):
**Finance intelligently. Control predictively. Deliver successfully.**

## 2. What each book must be

- The official knowledge foundation for its certification **and** a standalone professional reference.
- Aligned to the final certification blueprint and competency framework (`<book>/COMPETENCY_MAP.md`).
- Foundation-to-leadership depth; theory + practice + judgment + governance + leadership.
- Responsible AI integrated in every domain — never confined to one chapter.
- Globally applicable, cross-industry, jurisdiction-neutral; localization-ready (Arabic/RTL prepared).
- Distributed as a secured, student-watermarked PDF and web-readable content through the platform's
  document system.

## 3. Non-negotiable quality rules

1. Research before writing; original wording, examples, diagrams and questions only.
2. Cite authoritative sources; distinguish fact, recommended practice, professional judgment and PCI
   interpretation.
3. Never invent standards, laws, company facts, project data, quotations or citations.
4. Never imply endorsement by any company, standards body or institute; fictitious or properly
   anonymised examples unless reliable public sources support named cases.
5. Verify every mathematical result independently; decimal arithmetic for financial calculations;
   state assumptions, units, dates, currencies and rounding.
6. Never present one jurisdiction's legal, tax or accounting treatment as universal.
7. Version every chapter; retain editorial history; technical + editorial approval before publication.
8. Never expose secure exam questions; educational badges are never conflated with accredited
   qualifications.
9. Never generate the books in one uncontrolled pass — phase gates are mandatory (section 8).
10. Page count is an output check, not a writing method: no oversized type, whitespace padding,
    duplicated text, filler, repeated definitions, inflated appendices or one-paragraph pages.

## 4. Editorial voice

The validated PCP-AI voice: professional international **British English**; clear, complete sentences;
confident but qualified claims; practical examples; neutral global perspective; active voice where
appropriate. Prohibited: generic AI-generated introductions, repetitive summaries, empty motivational
language, excessive bullet lists, unexplained acronyms, unsupported superlatives, constant references to
"the exam", fabricated quotations. Trademark symbols (™/®) are **not** used in PCI branding (current
platform policy); third-party marks are acknowledged once, on the copyright page.

## 5. Shared infrastructure (single source of truth)

| Registry | File | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Terminology | `registries/TERMINOLOGY.md` | A term is defined once; used identically in both books and PCP-AI; silent redefinition is a defect |
| Formulas & symbols | `registries/FORMULAS.md` | One symbol table across both books; clashes resolved by explicit context rules; every formula has a verified golden example |
| Sources & citations | `registries/SOURCES.md` | Every citation resolves to a registry entry with edition/version, access date and rights status; claim-to-source mapping required for high-risk claims |
| Figures & illustrations | `registries/FIGURES.md` | Every figure numbered, captioned, sourced ("PCI original"), alt-texted, cross-referenced |

## 6. Concurrent-agent production rules

One chief editorial coordinator controls master outlines, terminology, citations, cross-references,
file ownership and publication gates. Bounded roles (architecture, research, domain authors per book,
responsible-AI specialist, ethics/governance, calculation verification, case studies, visuals,
templates, MCQs, independent technical review, citation/originality review, legal-risk review, copy
edit, accessibility/localization, typesetting, automated QA).

- **File ownership:** one stable chapter ID and one owner per manuscript file; no two agents edit the
  same file simultaneously.
- **Submission:** every author submits sources, assumptions and unresolved questions with the draft.
- **Review:** calculations and high-risk technical claims get independent review; numerical MCQs get
  two reviewers.
- **Acceptance:** content is never merged because it is long; duplicated, generic, unsupported or
  low-value content is rejected. Accept/revise/reject decisions are recorded in the phase reports.

## 7. Quality gates (every chapter)

Pattern conformance · competency alignment · technical accuracy · calculation verification · source
verification · originality review · style review · legal/company-mention review · diagram/table review ·
accessibility review · cross-reference check · MCQ validation · final editorial approval. A chapter
containing placeholder citations, unresolved calculations, duplicated prose or generic filler is
rejected at gate.

## 8. Phase gates

Phase 0 governance → Phase 1 prototypes (one domain per book, fully typeset and reviewed) → Phase 2
foundations → Phase 3 core technical batches → Phase 4 leadership/governance/AI → Phase 5 cases,
exercises, companions → Phase 6 independent review → Phase 7 copy edit & typesetting → Phase 8 pilot &
release. **No phase may be skipped to meet page count.** Each phase ends with the standard report
(sections completed; word/page estimates; competencies covered; sources added; calculations validated;
figures/tables; MCQs/exercises; gate results; similarity findings; open expert decisions; files
changed; next batch).

## 9. Legal and safety safeguards

Educational-purpose disclaimers in both books; PFL-AI additionally carries finance, accounting, tax,
legal and investment disclaimers and jurisdiction caveats. Originality/similarity review before
release; no reproduction of protected standards, competitor tables, diagrams or question banks; no
unlicensed images; a corrections channel is published; permissions and licences recorded in
`registries/SOURCES.md`. AI-assisted text always receives human editorial and technical review and is
never attributed to a named expert who did not author it.

## 10. Definition of done

A book is complete only when: it follows the validated pattern (departures documented and approved);
finished typeset length ≥ 1,200 meaningful pages; all blueprint competencies covered at required depth;
every chapter technically and editorially approved; every calculation independently validated; all
figures/tables original or licensed; citations valid; originality confirmed; AI content governed;
cases, exercises and MCQs complete; companion templates calculate correctly; glossary, references and
index complete; PDF accessible and professionally rendered; web version usable; page references and
cross-links work; student watermarking tested; corrected sources and final outputs archived with
versions.
