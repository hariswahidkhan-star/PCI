## Part One — Foundations (Domains 1–4)

### Domain 1 — Foundations of project finance leadership
- **KA 1.1 — The project finance leader:** 1.1.1 the role across the lifecycle · 1.1.2 corporate
  finance vs project finance (recourse spectrum) · 1.1.3 stakeholder objectives (sponsor, lender,
  government, operator, community) · 1.1.4 the infrastructure-finance market: asset classes, investors
  and capital flows
- **KA 1.2 — Value, cash and risk:** 1.2.1 value creation in projects · 1.2.2 cash as the binding
  constraint · 1.2.3 the risk-return-bankability triangle
- **KA 1.3 — Ethics, fiduciary awareness and responsible AI:** 1.3.1 fiduciary and professional
  obligations · 1.3.2 conflicts and independence · 1.3.3 the PCI responsible-AI principle in finance

### Domain 2 — Accounting and financial-statement foundations
- **KA 2.1 — The accrual model:** accrual concepts · recognition · the statements as one system
- **KA 2.2 — The three statements:** income statement · balance sheet · cash-flow statement · linkage
- **KA 2.3 — Project-relevant treatments:** working capital · revenue and cost recognition · capex vs
  opex · provisions and contingencies (IFRS discussed by name, own words, jurisdiction-neutral)
- **KA 2.4 — Ratio interpretation and project interfaces:** financial-ratio reading · project
  accounting interfaces with corporate reporting

### Domain 3 — Time value of money and financial mathematics *(quantitative flagship)*
- **KA 3.1 — Interest and value:** simple and compound interest · present and future value · discount
  factors
- **KA 3.2 — Annuities and schedules:** annuities and perpetuities · loan schedules (amortising,
  annuity, bullet) · compounding frequency and effective rates
- **KA 3.3 — Real-world adjustments:** nominal vs real (Fisher) · inflation and escalation · currency
  effects
- Every formula carries an independently verified golden example (formula registry).

### Domain 4 — Investment appraisal and capital budgeting *(quantitative)*
- **KA 4.1 — Discounted measures:** NPV · IRR (and its pathologies) · MIRR
- **KA 4.2 — Complementary measures:** payback · discounted payback · profitability index ·
  equivalent annual value
- **KA 4.3 — Decision contexts:** capital rationing · mutually exclusive investments · decision
  limitations and judgment
