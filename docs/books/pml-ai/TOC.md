# PML-AI Body of Knowledge — Detailed Table of Contents (Phase 0)

**Book:** PCI Project Management Leader – AI Body of Knowledge
**Structure:** 4 parts · 16 domains · 63 knowledge areas, numbered `Domain.KA.Topic` per the pattern
spec. Every domain follows the family chapter shape (section 4 of `PCI_BOOK_PATTERN_SPEC.md`): *Why this
domain exists* + learning objectives → Knowledge Areas → Advanced topics → Case study(ies) → Executive
perspective → Calculation exercises (quantitative domains) → Practitioner's toolkit → Exam preparation →
Domain summary. Responsible-AI treatment appears **in every domain** (an "AI in this domain" section
plus per-KA boxes where distinct), with Domain 14 carrying the systematic treatment.

**Page plan:** front matter 25–32 pp · 16 domains ≈ 70–78 pp each · appendices + glossary + formula
sheet + references + index 150–180 pp → planned typeset total ≈ **1,300–1,420 pp** (page count is an
output check, not a writing method).

## Part Two — Delivering the work (Domains 5–10)

### Domain 5 — Scope, requirements and value definition
KAs: 5.1 requirements discovery → 5.2 scope definition and acceptance criteria → 5.3 traceability,
validation and scope creep → 5.4 product thinking, design maturity, AI-assisted requirements with human
verification. Toolkit: requirements traceability matrix · acceptance-criteria patterns.

### Domain 6 — Planning, scheduling and delivery flow *(quantitative flagship)*
KAs: 6.1 planning levels and logic networks → 6.2 critical path and float (full CPM machinery) → 6.3
resources, constraints, milestones, rolling wave → 6.4 agile/predictive/hybrid schedules, recovery,
forecasting and scenario analysis. Master worked project opens here (single schedule and cost baseline
reused through Domains 6–8). Calculation exercises: CPM passes, float, compression trade-offs.

### Domain 7 — Cost, resources and commercial awareness *(quantitative)*
KAs: 7.1 estimating and budgeting → 7.2 cost baseline and forecasts → 7.3 earned value for leaders
(restating the family EVM symbols) → 7.4 resource economics, procurement strategy, contract models,
cash-flow awareness. Calculation exercises: EVM suite, EAC selection, cash-flow phasing.

### Domain 8 — Risk, uncertainty and resilience *(quantitative)*
KAs: 8.1 threats, opportunities, identification → 8.2 qualitative and quantitative analysis (EMV,
decision trees, ranges) → 8.3 contingency and reserves → 8.4 resilience, crisis leadership, cognitive
bias, AI-enabled risk sensing. Calculation exercises: EMV, decision trees, contingency sizing.

### Domain 9 — Quality, assurance and continuous improvement
KAs: 9.1 quality planning → 9.2 assurance and control → 9.3 acceptance, nonconformance, root-cause
analysis → 9.4 lessons learned, continuous improvement, data quality and AI-output quality.

### Domain 10 — Procurement, contracts and supply networks
KAs: 10.1 make-or-buy and procurement lifecycle → 10.2 tendering and evaluation → 10.3 contract
strategy and supplier governance → 10.4 claims awareness, change and disputes, ethical sourcing,
