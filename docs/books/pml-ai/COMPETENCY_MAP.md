**Certification:** PML-AI — PCI Project Management Leader – AI (`backend/Data/MultiCert.cs`, id 3)
**Blueprint status:** the platform publishes **no examination blueprint** for PML-AI (no domain
weightings, no item counts; `CertPage.cs` states these are "confirmed during application"; item counts
await a job-task analysis and the pass mark a modified-Angoff study). The authoritative
competency-adjacent content is the catalogue's published **24-item competency list** ("What you'll

the book, and (b) proposes an **indicative study weighting** by part — a programme proposal requiring
PCI approval before any examination use (outstanding decision OD-2).

## 2. Indicative study weighting by part (programme proposal — OD-2)

Responsible-AI treatment is distributed (every domain's AI sections + D14's systematic treatment), so
the AI share is measured across the whole surface — the PCL-AI precedent. Weights attach to parts, not
individual domains, mirroring the published PCL-AI blueprint convention.

## 3. Depth targets

Every KA is authored to the family checklist (spine §10): definitions with real frameworks named;
principles; formulae with verified worked examples where quantitative; pitfalls; AI treatment;
key-terms box; tagged MCQs; self-checks; cross-references. Leadership-level depth (advanced topics +
executive perspective) is mandatory in all 16 domains — this is a *Leader* credential.
