# Shared Source & Citation Registry — PML-AI · PFL-AI

**Rule:** every framework, standard or source named in either book resolves to a row here. Chapters cite
by name in prose (the PCL-AI convention — no numeric footnotes); this registry holds the bibliographic
record: publisher, edition/version, what the books may say about it, and rights status. **No invented
citations; no reproduction of protected text, tables, diagrams or question banks.** High-risk claims
(legal, tax, accounting, safety) additionally carry a claim-to-source mapping in the chapter's
submission notes.

Citation style: name the framework inline ("under IAS 37 a provision is recognised when…"), describe the
principle in the book's own words, and never quote protected text. Access dates and editions are
recorded here, not in running prose.

## 2. Prohibited-use register

- Proprietary bodies of knowledge, question banks, tables and diagrams of PMI, AACE, IPMA, CFA
  Institute, ACCA, CIMA, ISO publishers, universities and commercial textbooks: **never reproduced,
  closely paraphrased, or structurally mirrored.**
- No implication of endorsement/affiliation by any named body; the copyright page carries the standing
  non-affiliation notice (PCL-AI pattern).
- Real companies/projects only with verified public sources, cited, and no invented confidential
  details; otherwise fictitious, clearly-labelled examples.
